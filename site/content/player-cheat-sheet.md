{{< cheat-card "Rolling dice" >}}
*“If something can go wrong, roll the dice.”*

**Action Roll:** Choose an Attribute and Skill; describe why they fit.

**Reaction Roll:** The Director chooses your Attribute and Skill.

**Any Skill** can pair with any Attribute.
{{< /cheat-card >}}

{{< cheat-card "Advantage & disadvantage" >}}
Luck, Feats and gear can change your chances.

**+1:** Add one die.  **−1:** Remove one die.

### Help
The Director grants one:
- **+1** to your roll.
- Automatic success.
- The ability to attempt the roll.

### Hindrance
The Director imposes one:
- **−1** to your roll.
- Automatic failure.
- You need Help to attempt the roll.

Other Adventurers can forgo their roll to Help you.

> **Spend 1 Luck** to gain **+1** on an Action or Reaction Roll.
{{< /cheat-card >}}
{{< /cheat-column >}}

{{< cheat-card "Re-rolls" >}}
With at least one Basic Success, you may re-roll all dice outside a combination.
- **Better result:** keep it.
- **No improvement:** lose one previous success of your choice.

> **Better = an extra success or an upgraded success.**

### Free Re-roll
- When available, replaces your normal re-roll. Always worth taking.
- Never risks your initial successes.
- Allowed even with no successes on your first roll.

### All In
Only after a re-roll or Free Re-roll improves your result, you may roll the dice outside combinations once more.
- **Improves again:** keep it.

> **No improvement: lose ALL previous successes.**
{{< /cheat-card >}}

{{< cheat-card "Facing enemies" >}}
Opponents count as one Enemy—even a group. Combat begins when an Enemy enters the scene.

**Alternate these turns:**\
**Action Turn:** Each Adventurer takes an action and one free Quick Action. Players choose their order. Take the Quick Action before or after the Action Roll.\
**Reaction Turn:** The Director describes the Enemy’s actions. All Adventurers defend with a Reaction Roll.

### Initiative
- Adventurers attack first: start with an Action Turn.
- Enemy attacks first: start with a Reaction Turn.
{{< /cheat-card >}}

{{< cheat-card "Dangerous rolls" >}}
A roll is **Dangerous** if failure costs Grit.

| Difficulty | Grit lost |
| :--- | :---: |
| Basic | 1 |
| Critical | 3 |
| Extreme | 9 |
| Impossible | ALL |
{{< /cheat-card >}}

{{< cheat-card "Damage control" >}}
After a failed Dangerous Roll, use smaller successes to reduce Grit loss:
- Each Basic Success saves **1 Grit**.
- Each Critical Success saves **3 Grit**.

> **No Damage Control on Impossible rolls.**
