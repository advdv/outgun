

Three = One One = Three 1 higher success is worth as much as 3 lower successes, and 3 lower successes are worth as much as 1 higher success. 

This means you can spend 1 Critical Success like 3 Basic Successes, and 1 Extreme Success like 3 Critical Successes. 

Conversely, you can combine 3 Basic Successes into 1 Critical Success, and 3 Critical Successes into 1 Extreme Success.
