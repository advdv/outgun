og adventure<sup>is the first Genre Book for the cinematic action roleplaying game</sup> outgunned<sup>. This book is a brand-new format, different from both World of</sup> Killers and Action Flicks, and it’s not your typical expansion but an example of what you can expect from outgunned in the future. 

But before looking at the details, let me ask you a question: do you already own the outgunned<sup>corebook??</sup> 

# **Yes, I have the** outgunned **corebook.** 

You can consider og adventure  like a mega-expansion, a rulebook that changes some of the standard outgunned mechanics and enriches your gaming experience with an unprecedented number of new options, even more than in World of Killers. Everything you need to bring grandiose pulp adventures to outgunned<sup>is contained in this book, including a rich chapter dedicated to</sup> 

## the **supernatural** . 

But there’s more! og adventure offers simplified rules for combat and chase scenes, while also adding new concepts such as traps, getaways, and multiple dangers. You will be able to choose which elements to use for your sessions, picking either from classic outgunned or the new og adventure. All fully compatible. Customize your gaming experience! 

# **No, I do not own** outgunned. 

The good news is that og adventure is completely **standalone** . You don’t need any other book to play, not even the outgunned corebook. In og adventure<sup>, you will find everything you need to create Adventurers and set</sup> off on your search for the treasure. 

But if you want to make use of the detailed tools to manage adrenaline-paced action scenes, consider that the outgunned corebook contains all advanced mechanics to manage chases, enemies, firearms, and ranges. Not to mention 10 unique Roles, such as the Sleuth, the Agent, or the Spy, 18 Tropes, and a slew of Traits for both Adventurers and their Enemies. All perfectly compatible with og adventure<sup>.</sup>
