# What’s at stake? 

When facing a roll there is always something on the line. Having a clear image of what’s at stake allows the Director to determine what the **consequences** of a success or failure should be. 

Broadly speaking, a nice **success** always means you reached your goal. You’ve done it once again! 

A **failure** , on the other hand, means things didn’t really go as planned. You win some, you lose some. 

# Attributes and Skills 

When you’re making a roll, you must always use one Attribute and one Skill. Although Skills are listed under the Attribute they are most often paired with, bear in mind that **you can always use any Skill with any Attribute.** 

When shooting, you’ll often roll Nerves+Shoot, and when throwing punches, you’ll roll Brawn+Fight, but you can also use your imagination. 

To hit a goon after sneaking up behind them, you can roll Crime+Fight. To find a way out while you’re driving, you can roll Nerves+Awareness. To make a good impression on someone with your sophisticated taste, you can roll Smooth+Know. 

# Adventure Dice 

**Outgunned Adventure** is meant to be played with special 6-sided dice. Each face on these dice sports an original symbol to make it easier to recognize successes. 

The **Adventure Dice** from are not needed to play, but they make for a more tailored experience. Alternatively, you can use normal 6-sided dice.
