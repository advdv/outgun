

# What could go wrong? 

If you’re ever in doubt about which Attribute or Skill to use for a roll, ask yourself “What could go wrong?”. 

When you have a clear idea of the consequences of your failure, it will be easier to think about a way to 

avoid them. You’re driving at top speed toward a bridge and see it is rising and opening up. What can go wrong here? 

You could chicken out and stomp on the brakes at the very last second. This means you should roll Nerves+Cool.
