# **<mark>`SCENE 5 INT. VIKING SHIP - DAY`</mark>** 

```
On one side, the bow of the ship is deeply wedged into the ice.
On the other, the stern is gingerly resting on a rock spur. The
entirety of the body of the Viking drakkar is suspended in the
void, and could fall in the chasm at any moment.
```

Maybe a thousand years ago this chasm was filled with water. `What’s certain, is that the ADVENTURERS won’t have an easy time climbing aboard given the current conditions.` 

```
This Scene is divided in 4 main phases, but it’s extremely free-
form, and there are several ways to go about it.
```

`1. The ADVENTURERS need to` **`get on the ship`** `. For example, they could use ropes and grappling hooks, build an improvised bridge, or climb on one of the ice walls. Depending on their approach, they will need to make Basic or Critical rolls.` 

`2.` Once on the ship, the ADVENTURERS must find a way to **`get below deck`** `and reach the inner hold door. In this phase, they must choose their moves with extreme caution, possibly making Stealth rolls to avoid compromising the ship’s delicate balance.` 

`3.` Below deck, the ADVENTURERS find a **`heavy trapdoor`** `that can be forced open with lockpicks or brute strength thanks to a` **`Critical Action Roll`** `.` 

`4.` In the cargo hold of the ship, the ADVENTURERS find nothing by old crates full of frozen fish and other trivial items. In order to proceed, they must find **`the secret passage to the treasure`** `.` 

**11** `The problem is that the Viking ship is extremely unstable.` **`Whenever the ADVENTURERS fail a roll, the ship will roll to one side as a consequence, possibly even turning upside down`** `, getting ever-closer to the point when it falls into the chasm.` 

`Whenever the ship shifts, all ADVENTURERS must face a` **`Critical Reaction Roll`** `and run the risk of losing some Grit. Additionally, when the ship is on one side or upside-down, the` ADVENTURERS will have a hard time finding their bearings, and `could be forced to` **`climb back`** `to the hold instead of continuing their descent, as well as having to deal with all problems that could follow.`
