## Luck & Adrenaline 

In **Outgunned** , Heroes can rely on Adrenaline, while Adventurers in **Outgunned Adventure** can rely on Luck. But what’s the difference? 

As far as the game is concerned, **Luck and Adrenaline are interchangeable** . Each Feat that requires you to spend Adrenaline can also be activated by spending Luck, and vice versa! The main difference between these resources is the way they are granted and their impact on the narrative. 

You will find more details on the topic in **Outgunned Adventure** . 



# Grit 

Grit is what allows you to run through the traps while everyone else joins the mountain of skeletons. It’s only thanks to your Grit that you can stay inches ahead of a rolling boulder without getting squashed! 

Your **12 boxes of Grit** are the only things between you and death, so hang onto them. We will talk more about them in the upcoming chapter **TRAPS & DANGERS.** 

Once you’re out of Grit, you run the risk of being **Left for Dead** , and the only thing that can save you is a spin of the **Death Roulette** . It will become harder and harder to bring home your bacon. 



# You Look 

In the **You Look** section of your Sheet, you can note down the Conditions you get on your adventures. 

**Conditions** impose -1 to specific dice rolls, and can be accumulated as consequence of your failures. Each Condition can be obtained and removed in a different way, but we will not go into further details in this QuickStart.
