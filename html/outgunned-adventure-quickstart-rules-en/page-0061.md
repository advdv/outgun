# _Odin’s Route_ 

```
Erik the Red wanted to ensure that only a true Viking could make it
past his traps, and this is why Odin’s Route hides a clever catch!
```

`If an Adventurer looks through Odin’s Key with their left eye (the same side covered by the golden eye-patch in the statue)` they will easily spot a path on the tiles… but alas, it will be `the` **`wrong one!`** `It will only take three steps, barely enough to draw a sigh of relief, before the trap triggers.` 

Historically, **`Odin always wears a patch on his right eye`** `. Erik the Red placed it wrong on the statue in order to mislead future grave-robbers! The Adventurers can see through this trick thanks to their own knowledge, or by making a roll in Focus+Know at the right time. Always reward players for their intuition and caution, maybe with a nice Spotlight! By looking through Odin’s Key` with their right eye, an Adventurer can finally find the right path `through the hallway.`
