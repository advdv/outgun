# **<mark>`SCENE 6 INT. TREASURE HOLD - DAY`</mark>** 

```
The hold of the ship is full of crates and rotting barrels, but
there’s no trace of a treasure or anything valuable left by Erik
the Red. Looking around, it appears as if all their searching led
to a hole in the water, but the Viking ship is obviously hiding
one last secret: a false bottom.
```

The ADVENTURERS can find the false bottom leading to the treasure hold by moving some of the planks in the floor, or by making a `successful roll in Focus+Detect. Alternatively, when a failure` causes the ship to roll for the first time, a large crack forms in `the wood, causing old and worn gold coins to scatter through the hold and revealing the path to the treasure.`

```
After opening a passage to the false bottom of the ship, the
ADVENTURERS can see a mountain of gold coins and other precious
items: a real, actual treasure.
```

```
But the Adventurers didn’t come here to look for gold, they are
after something far more precious.
```

This must be the artifact Wieloch is looking for. There are only two words on the letter the skeleton is clutching` _`“` Ἀτλαντὶς νῆσος_ _`”`_ `, which is Greek for` **`Island of Atlantis`** `.`

**14** 

`After retrieving the cypher, the ADVENTURERS have no time to celebrate, as the ship begins to creak and crack, clearly on the` verge of starting its final descent to the bottom of the chasm. `There’s no time to lose.` 

`To make it off the ship before it’s too late, the ADVENTURERS will have to succeed in three different : one to get out of the hold` **`[Basic]`** `, one to get off the ship` **`[Critical]`** `, and one to` find and reach a safe place **`[Critical]`** `. The exact rolls depend on their approach to the situation.`
