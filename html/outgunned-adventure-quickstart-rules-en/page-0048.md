# Traps 

You open the door and trigger a trap. You pull the lever and trigger a trap. You grab the treasure and trigger a trap. You get in and trigger a trap. You don’t get in… and trigger a trap! You don’t need me to tell you that you will find many traps on your path to adventure. The good news is, that in og adventure  you’ll find a long list of traps ready to use.
