Now that I have taught you everything you need to become a great adventurer, it’s time to put your skills to the test with some hands-on action. Close this guide, and get ready to set off on your first adventure! 

**The upcoming pages go over an introductory Shot and should be read only by Directors.** So, if you’re not one of them, stop reading, you’ll spoil the plot and fun for yourself. 

> Shot Summary ~~oR~~ <mark>aa</mark> ~~ee~~ In Frozen Legacy, the Adventurers must find a path through the frigid ice of **Greenland** to search for the legendary tomb of the Viking explorer **Erik the Red** . 

- ♦ **Scene 1:** The Adventurers climb an imposing wall of ice and reach a mausoleum that looks like it could be the tomb of Erik the Red. 

- ♦ **Scene 2:** The fake Christian tomb of Erik the Red turns out to be a cunning trap. The Adventurers need to avoid it or deal with it in order to find a secret passage through the water. 

- ♦ **Scene 3:** In an underground cave, the Adventurers must face Odin’s challenge to prove that they are worthy of the hidden treasure. 

- ♦ **Scene 4/5:** After making it through the secret passage, the Adventurers find a huge abyss that plunges into the depths of the earth. Nestled at the heart of this abyss, suspended in the void, there is a massive Viking ship. The treasure is in the hull of that ship, but a single misstep could cause everything to crumble. 

- ♦ **Scene 6:** Inside the ship, the Adventurers finally find the coveted treasure, a **map** to the lost city of **Atlantis** . But there’s no time to celebrate, as the ship starts to fall into the abyss, and the Adventurers must rush to get away before it’s too late.
