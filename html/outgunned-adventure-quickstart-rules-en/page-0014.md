# **Adventurer Sheet** 

**Adventurers** are men and women of action who face great risks because they’re on a quest to find an invaluable **treasure** . Their lives are always hanging by a thread, as they face terrible odds. 

Some Adventurers are professors in a hat who punch nazis while trying to save the World. Others are hopeful youngsters in search for their past, or librarians too curious for their own good. Others still are heirs of wealthy families, ready to risk it all for a treasure. Some are even tomb robbers and smugglers who end up involved in something greater than themselves. 

But they all have something in common. Their eyes are always on the treasure.
