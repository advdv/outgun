# Hero and Adventurer 

In **Outgunned Adventure** , the main characters are called Adventurers, while in Outgunned, they are called Heroes. 

As far as the rules are concerned, “Heroes” and “Adventurers” are interchangeable terms, so all rules and Traits that concern Heroes, work just as well for Adventurers, and vice versa.
