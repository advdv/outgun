You can try to talk with your Enemies, but they will never listen. These goons and dumb henchmen only understand two words: fists and bullets. 

If you want to save the day and prove you are an Adventurer worthy of an action movie, you better learn how to fight. 

In og adventure  you’ll find several tools to personalize your gaming experience and set the stage for thrilling fights and breathtaking chases. For now, I’ll try to teach you the basics at least. 

## Combat <mark>a SRR</mark> 

# <mark>eee</mark> 

During combat, regardless of whether it’s a shoot-out or a nice fist-fight, there are two alternating turns: 

**Action Turn:** Adventurers are free to take any action they want and make the relative Action Rolls. Additionally, all Adventurers also can take a Quick Action for free. 

**Jason:** _I jump out from cover and open fire on the goons._ 

**Reaction Turn:** The Director states the Enemies’ actions and all Heroes must defend themselves with a Reaction Roll ; . **Regista:** _The goons come toward you with guns blazing. Everyone, roll Brawn+Stunt to duck out of the way._
