

# Classic Rivals 

## **THE WARLORD** 

_A tough guy who follows a strict moral code and can rely on the unwavering loyalty of their soldiers._ 

**Strong Spots:** Private Army. Extraordinary individual. Survivor. **Weak Spots:** Terminally ill. Doesn’t want to look like a coward. **Desire:** Seeks immortality. 

## **THE NAZI OCCULTIST** 

_A slimy colonel who is also a staunch believer in supernatural forces._ **Strong Spots:** Private Army. Occult Knowledge. **Weak Spots:** Afraid of the ghosts from their past. **Desire:** Wants to become the new Führer. 

## **THE AMBITIOUS PROFESSOR** 

_A narcissistic archeologist who knows the Treasure better than everybody else._ **Strong Spots:** Luminary. Control of the Territory. **Weak Spots:** Afraid of their sponsors. Lied to the natives. **Desire:** Wants to become filthy rich. 

## **THE YOUNG HEIR** 

_An ambitious youngster who wants to prove themselves to the World._ **Strong Spots:** Unlimited funding. Private Army. **Weak Spots:** Self-doubting. **Desire:** Displaying the Treasure like a trophy. 

## **THE REBEL LEADER** 

_A war veteran who bears many scars and wants to see the World burn._ **Strong Spots:** Survivor. Luminary. Private Army. **Weak Spots:** Betrayed a friend. Deaf in one ear. **Desire:** Wants to start a war. 

## **THE OLD FRIEND** 

_A ghost from the past, who came back to haunt the Adventurers._ **Strong Spots:** Leverage on the Adventurers. Unlimited funding. **Weak Spots:** In love with one Adventurer. **Desire:** Seeks vengeance.
