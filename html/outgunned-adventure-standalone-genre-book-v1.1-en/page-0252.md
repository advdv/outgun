# **<mark>`SCENE 6 - INT. TREASURE HOLD - DAY`</mark>** 

```
The hold of the ship is full of crates and rotting barrels, but
there’s no trace of a treasure or anything valuable left by Erik
the Red. Looking around, it appears as if all their searching led
to a hole in the water, but the Viking ship is obviously hiding
one last secret: a hidden false bottom.
```

The ADVENTURERS can find the false bottom leading to the treasure hold by moving some of the planks in the floor, or by making a `successful roll in Focus+Detect. Alternatively, when a failure` causes the ship to roll for the first time, a large crack forms `in the wood, causing old and worn gold coins to scatter through the hold and revealing the path to the treasure.` 

**12** 

`After opening a passage to the false bottom of the ship, the AD-` VENTURERS find a mountain of gold coins and other precious items: `a real, actual` **`treasure`** `.` 

```
But the ADVENTURERS didn’t come here to look for gold, they are
after a far greater treasure.
```

```
Waiting for them at the bottom of the hold, there’s the skeleton
of a Viking warrior holding a letter and a cypher disk with a
trident engraving. This must be the artifact WIELOCH is look-
ing for.
```

`There are only two words on the letter the skeleton is clutching` _`“` Ἀτλαντὶς νῆσος_ _`”`_ `, which is Greek for` **`Island of Atlantis`** `.` 

**13** `After retrieving the cypher disk, the ADVENTURERS have no time to celebrate, as the ship begins to creak and crack, clearly on the` verge of starting its final descent to the bottom of the chasm. `There’s no time to lose.` 

**14** 

`To leave the ship before it's too late, the ADVENTURERS will have to succeed in three different` **`Action Rolls`** `: one to get out of the hold` **`[Basic]`** `, one to get off the ship` **`[Critical]`** `, and one to` find and reach a safe place **`[Critical]`** `. The exact rolls depend on their approach to the situation.`
