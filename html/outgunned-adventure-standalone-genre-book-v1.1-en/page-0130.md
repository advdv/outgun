

# **Guns & Gear** 

As you’re sprinting, a chasm opens before you. Now you really wish you had a grappling hook. A hulking brute is beating your face like a drum, laughing with every punch. You can never find your pistol when you need it. You’ve been going in circles in this forest for hours. Ah, if only you had brought a compass! 

All in all, gear does not an Adventurer make, but when life throws impossible challenges and insurmountable obstacles in your path, sometimes you need all the help you can get. 

Fear not! This chapter will teach you all you need to know about: 

- ♦ **Gear:** The items you carry around while adventuring because they can Help you overcome obstacles along the way. 

- ♦ **Weapons:** The guns, rifles, and machetes that stand between you and your Enemies, and can sometimes make the difference between life and… the worse alternative. 

- ♦ **Gear and Weapon Feats:** Much like Adventurers, weapons and gear also have their specific peculiarities, which can be represented by Feats. 

- ♦ **Bag and Backpack:** With everything you need to go on an adventure, your pockets won’t be enough. Luckily you can rely on your bag and backpack to keep your gear in order. 

- ♦ **Key Items:** Some items come and go, but Keys are too important to be left lying around. 

- ♦ **Cash:** Money can’t buy happiness, but it can buy rations, weapons, and favors. Cash represents precisely the small stash you take with you, which you can spend to make your life easier. 

## HELP AND HiNDRANCE FROM GEAR 

Some weapons and pieces of gear allow you to take actions beyond your normal capabilities. For example, a pistol allows you to shoot, and a musical instrument allows you to play music. But most of these items grant you essential Help to certain actions on your adventures, and in some cases, they can even pose a Hindrance. 

As a general rule, **Help grants you +1 to a roll, while Hindrance causes -1** . If you want to read more information on this topic and to learn how to use Help and Hindrance in creative ways, you can go to the relevant chapter in **SECTION 1: ADVENTURERS** .
