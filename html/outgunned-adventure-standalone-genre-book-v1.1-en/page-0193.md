



# Weakness 

Some Supernatural Enemies, including Supernatural Rivals, have a weakness that the Adventurers can exploit in order to gain a crucial Help, to cancel out one of their Supernatural Feats, or to chase them off for a while. 

For example, some creatures from European folklore can be gravely injured by weapons made of “cold iron”, and some mummies even flee at the mere sight of a cat. I’m serious! 

The Director is tasked with establishing the weaknesses of a creature and giving the Adventurers a chance to learn about them through clues scattered throughout their adventure. 

Bear in mind that many Minions share at least one of their legendary master’s weaknesses, and that some Supernatural Rivals become immune to one or all of their weaknesses when they gain their full power during the Moment of Truth. 

## Holy Weapons 

There are mythical weapons forged in ancient times for the exact purpose of fighting against a Supernatural Enemy. 

If an Adventurer manages to get their hands on the right **holy weapon** , it always grants them a Help when fighting against a Supernatural Enemy or Rival. 

Holy weapons are one-of-a-kind artifacts that, as far as the rules are concerned, often coincide with a **Key** or even the **Treasure** . 

In some cases, the Director can establish that the holy weapon can only be wielded by the **chosen one** , or that it can only be used to give the fatal blow to the Supernatural Enemy.
