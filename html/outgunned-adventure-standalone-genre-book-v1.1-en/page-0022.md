# Roles 



The Role of an Adventurer is a key feature that tells you what kind of person they are, what they can do, and probably also how they spent their life before this journey. This is a crucial choice! 

The first thing to do when creating your Adventurer, is choosing their Role: 

- ♦ **The Daredevil** 

- ♦ **The Guardian** 

- ♦ **The Captain** 

- ♦ **The Hunter** 

- ♦ **The Heart** 

- ♦ **The Star** 

- ♦ **The Professor** 

- ♦ **The Technician** 

- ♦ **The Scoundrel** 

- ♦ **The Smuggler** 

When you choose a Role, you gain **1 point in an Attribute and 1 point each in 10 Skills.** Then, you can choose **2 Feats** from the list of 6 for the Role. 

Additionally, your Role also gives you some **starting Gear** , and suggests **Backgrounds** , **Catchphrases** , and **Flaws** you can use as inspiration to fill in your Personal Data. 

If you ask me, I recommend you think first and foremost about what you want to play, and how you want to tackle your next adventure. 

If you like the idea of being the only one in the group who understands, or at least tries to understand what is going on, you could play as a Professor. But if you prefer facing your problems head-on and laughing in the face of danger, you should choose the Daredevil. 

If you see no problem in blowing up an ancient pillar to create a bridge in a handful of seconds, the Technician is who you're looking for. 

Anyway, always remember that you should never have two Adventurers playing the same Role, unless it is unavoidable. 

## Other Roles 

In the outgunned corebook you can find 10 Roles perfect for any occasion, like the Agent, the Nobody, and the Criminal. 

When playing adventure, you are always free to use all the Roles and Tropes from outgunned and other expansions if you feel they would be appropriate.
