The Star<br><!-- End of picture text -->

_A cabaret singer, a wealthy patron of the arts, a world-famous actor, or an upper-cruster in search of a thrill._ 

Stars are used to being the center of attention, always surrounded by raving fans, social climbers, and suitors looking to catch their eye. 

When a Star goes on an adventure, they are trying to prove a point, to win a bet, or to discover what happened to someone dear to them. But most often, these Adventurers are thrown into the action by chance, and they will have to learn how to get their hands dirty and smile in the face of adversities.
