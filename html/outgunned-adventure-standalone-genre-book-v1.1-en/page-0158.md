# _Classic Deterrent Traps_ 

## _Blade Tunnel_ 

_The next hallway appears safe enough, at least until you see blades well over three feet in length drop from the ceiling and start swinging._ 

When the trap triggers, the corridor or tunnel fills with rotating `or swinging blades that threaten to slice down anyone trying to walk past them.` 

`Depending on the distance that needs to be crossed, making it through the tunnel could require 1 to 3 Dangerous rolls with` Critical difficulty. In some cases, the first Adventurer who makes `it across the tunnel can deactivate the trap to help the others.` 

```
In case of failure, the Adventurers manage to move forward but
the blades graze them, causing them to lose Grit. If an Adven-
turer fails two consecutive rolls, the Director can also hit
them with one Condition of their choice between Scared, Nervous,
or Hurt.
```
