# TROPES 



The Trope is the archetype of your Adventurer, and it allows you to get a quick look into their character and mannerisms. As in every good movie, some Adventurers are Wild at Heart and live in close contact with nature, and others are Salty Dogs with sea water running through their veins. Some Adventurers are Cowardly Lions who will try their best to hide, until they finally show everyone what they’re made of. 

Once you’ve settled on a Role for your Adventurer, you must choose one of these 15 Tropes: 

|**Action Archeologist**|**Detestable Bastard**|**Parental Figure**|
|---|---|---|
|**Adventuring Author**|**Dreamer**|**Proper Gentleman/Lady**|
|**Born Rebel**|**Flying Steamroller**|**Reluctant Hero**|
|**Cold and Distant**|**Gentle Giant**|**Salty Dog**|
|**Cowardly Lion**|**Indispensable Rogue**|**Wild at Heart**|



When you choose a Trope, you gain **1 point in an Attribute and 1 point each in 8 Skills** . Then, you can choose **1 Feat** from the list of 4 for the Trope. Additionally, each Trope gives you an interesting prompt about how to portray your Adventurer. 

You’ll see that each Trope is connected to 2 Attributes. You're free to choose either of them when assigning your Attribute Point. For example, if you choose to play the Wild at Heart Trope, you gain 1 point in either Brawn or Nerves, your choice. 

If your Role has already given you a point in one of the Attributes associated with your Trope, then you must choose the other one. 

## Archetypes, Not Stereotypes 

Adhering to a Trope helps you dive straight into the action with a reasonably clear idea of how your Adventurer will act during the mission, and it also helps other players get an idea of who your Adventurer is and how to interact with them. But you must be careful not to take it too far. 

When playing as your Adventurer you should always keep in mind their Trope, but never adhere to it blindly. Even Adventurers are well-rounded characters, and there’s no need for them to become stereotypes or robots, incapable of change!
