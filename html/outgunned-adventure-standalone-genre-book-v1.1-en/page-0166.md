You could think that the life of an Adventurer is all a thrilling race toward the Treasure, with winds blowing in your sails and eyes always aimed at your objective. 

Everyone believes that, at least until they see a 100-ton boulder rolling toward them, or find themselves running away from a Nazi battalion, or even possibly come face to face with an angry screaming mummy emerging from a sandstorm. Yes, that happens sometimes. 

Regardless of how strong your motivation to reach your goal may be, you will often end up running in the other direction, and you better run in earnest. 

When the Adventurers are put to flight by a deadly trap or by a mob of Enemies that they can’t hope to fight, they will have to **run** . 

Run scenes are extremely fast-paced moments when the Adventurers need to rush in order to avoid certain death. Just that, they need to run, no time for looking back!
