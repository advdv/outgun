# _Classic Trigger Traps_ 

## _Wall Darts_ 

_`As soon as you set foot in the corridor, you hear an ominous hiss` and dozens of darts come flying out from the walls._ 

```
All Adventurers currently in the room or hallway must make a
Critical Reaction Roll  in Crime+Awareness in order to dodge
in time.
```

```
In case of failure, the darts graze the Adventurers, who lose an
appropriate amount of Grit. Additionally, the Director can de-
cide that the darts were poisoned, and anyone who fails to roll
at least a Basic Success now looks Poisoned.
```

## _Cleaver_ 

_A huge blade, sharp as a razor, starts swinging from one side of the hallway to the other, like a lethal pendulum that could cut you to ribbons._ 

```
The Adventurer who triggered the trap and everyone close to
them must make a Critical Reaction Roll  in Nerves+Stealth to
squeeze to one side and avoid being cut.
```

```
In case of failure, the blade misses the Adventurers, who get so
stressed that they still lose Grit. Additionally, the Adventurer
with the worst roll loses their Bag or Backpack, torn away by
the swinging cleaver.
```
