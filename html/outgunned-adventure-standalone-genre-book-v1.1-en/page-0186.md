# _The Abominable Yeti_ 

```
Among the snowy peaks of Nepal, explorers swear to have seen huge
footprints that belong to a monstrous creature. A beast as tall
as three humans, hailing from a forgotten age.
People call it the abominable snowman and, according to the leg-
ends, it’s the guardian of a cursed location where no human has
ever set foot.
```
