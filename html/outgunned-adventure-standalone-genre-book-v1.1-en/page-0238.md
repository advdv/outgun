In **Frozen Legacy** , the Adventurers must find a path through the frigid ice of **Greenland** to search for the legendary tomb of the Viking explorer **Erik the Red** . According to their patron, Professor Kasper Wieloch, there is a rare **artifact** hiding among the loot gathered by Erik the Red. 

- ♦ **Scene 1:** The Shot begins as the Adventurers climb an imposing wall of ice. Their effort is repaid when they finally reach the top and find a **mausoleum** that appears to be the tomb of Erik the Red. 

- ♦ **Scene 2:** The **fake Christian tomb** of Erik the Red turns out to be a cunning trap. The Adventurers need to avoid it or deal with it in order to find a secret passage through the water. 

- ♦ **Scene 3:** In an underground cave, the Adventurers must face Odin’s challenge to prove that they are worthy of the hidden treasure. Only true vikings have the knowledge that’s needed to avoid a **deadly trap** . 

- ♦ **Scene 4/5:** After making it through the secret passage, the Adventurers find a huge abyss that plunges into the depths of the earth. Nestled at the heart of this abyss, suspended in the void, there is a massive **Viking ship** . The treasure is in the hull of that ship, but a single misstep could cause everything to crumble. 

- ♦ **Scene 6:** Inside the ship, the Adventurers finally find the coveted artifact, a **cypher disk** that will lead them to the lost city of **Atlantis** . But there’s no time to celebrate, as the ship starts to fall into the abyss, and the Adventurers must rush to get away before it’s too late.
