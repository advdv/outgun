# _The Nagini_ 

```
According to Hindu and Buddhist tradition, the Naga are a divine
race of human-snake hybrids whose king watched over the Chin-
tamani Stone. Centuries ago, the Naga were defeated by the god
Garuda and relegated to the depths of the sea, and the Nagini
was turned into a stone statue, waiting for nothing more than a
chance to awaken and unleash her fury.
```

```
When the Nagini is returned to her original form, the ancient
Naga army and all snakes of land and sea will answer her call.
```
