# The Treasure’s true nature 

The Treasure is shrouded in mystery and hides many secrets that go deeper than you could imagine. 

But when the **Moment of Truth** comes, the Treasure finally reveals its true nature, manifesting its majestic and terrible power. 

Once they reach the final stage of their journey, the Adventurers must finally face an uncomfortable or dangerous truth that forces them to change course and prove just how pure their intentions are. 

For example: 

- ♦ **The Treasure is not what you thought:** All the stories you’ve heard about the Treasure were wrong. Now that you’ve laid eyes on it, the Treasure appears much more humble or mundane than you thought. The lesson it’s trying to teach and the message from the past it carries is worth more than gold. 

- ♦ **Death and destruction:** Whether it is a divine weapon or a vehicle for the spread of a lethal virus, the Treasure is too dangerous to be brought into the World. Anyone who tries to tame its power would be destroyed. 

- ♦ **A heavy price to pay:** The Treasure requires a terrible sacrifice in order to be taken or used. Are you willing to allow one of your friends to die in order to grab the Treasure before it sinks in a sea of lava, or to steal the life of someone else to gain immortality? 

- ♦ **You can’t take it away:** The entire region where the Treasure is located physically or spiritually depends on the Treasure being there. The right thing to do is to leave it to its rightful owners, or to take it back where it belongs. 

- ♦ **The Treasure doesn't exist:** After braving countless dangers, you learn that the Treasure never existed. It was only a silly legend, a dream of glory that, across the ages, led many Adventurers to their doom. What can you learn from this story?
