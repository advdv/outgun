Rival<br><!-- End of picture text -->

Your **Rival** is a ruthless individual who seeks the Treasure for their own evil goals, and they could succeed, if you weren’t there to stop them! When they say that the Treasure should not be allowed to fall into the wrong hands, the Rival is exactly who they talk about. As such, the true goal of every Adventurer worth their salt is not to take the Treasure for themselves, but to stop the Rival from taking it. 

Every Rival worthy of the name can rely on several **Strong Spots** , but also has **Weak Spots** that the Adventurers can use against them. And to paint a full picture, every Rival is moved by a **Desire** , a monomaniacal, evil, or simply crazed reason why they are trying to obtain the Treasure.
