# Enemy Attack 

When the Enemy attacks, all Adventurers involved in combat will have to make a Reaction Roll with difficulty equal to the Enemy Attack. 

For example, if an Enemy has Critical Attack, it means Adventurers will need to score a Critical Success to dodge or parry their strikes. Failing to do so will result in the Adventurers losing Grit, exactly as would happen with any other Dangerous Rolls. 

The Director can set the Enemy Attack as: 

- ♦ **Basic:** The Enemy is really nothing impressive, their blows miss the mark often. 

- ♦ **2 Basic:** The Enemy is a normal thug, or an untrained or ill-equipped individual. 

- ♦ **Critical:** The Enemy knows what they're doing, their blows can do some damage. Most Enemies worthy of the name have Critical Attack. 

- ♦ **2 Critical:** The Enemy is very strong, and it’s likely that their blows will do at least some damage. The Adventurers will often have to settle for some Damage Control. 

- ♦ **Extreme:** The Enemy has superhuman strength or lethal weapons. Withstanding their attacks is a heroic feat. 

When the Enemy is in a tight spot or in a disadvantageous position, the Director could also choose to lower their Attack score by 1 or 2 levels for a Turn. 

# Advanced Enemies 

In Section 6: SUPERNATURAL THREAT, you will find Supernatural Enemies. These threats will be more fearsome and complex than simple Enemies. 

In the Outgunned corebook you can also find tactical Enemies that can rely on exceptional Feats and break the flow of combat with their Special Action. 

If you want to add Enemy Feats or Special Actions from the other Outgunned books, you can assign up to 5 Feat Points or turn up to 3 Grit boxes into Hot Boxes. 

Similarly, if the Enemy manages to get the upper hand on the Adventurers or pulls out a missile-launcher, a stick of dynamite, or some other lethal weapon, the Director could also raise their Attack score by 1 or 2 levels for that Turn.
