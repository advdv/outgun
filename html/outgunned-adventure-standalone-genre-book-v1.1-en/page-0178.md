# List of Supernatural Feats 



Here you can find a list of Supernatural Feats, each with their Feat Points (FP) cost.  However, you are free to come up with your own original Feats, or even use the ones from other outgunned books. 

A Supernatural Enemy can rely on up to 6 Feat Points, while their Minions only have 2. 

## **BEAST [1 FP]** 

_The Enemy has a tough hide and can rely on sharp fangs or claws._ Adventurers suffer -1 when rolling to strike the Enemy bare-handed. 

## **BLIND DEVOTION [2 FP]** 

_When you meet the Enemy’s dull eyes, you realize there’s no human emotion._ Adventurers cannot scare, persuade, or bribe the Enemy in any way, they also suffer -1 when rolling to deceive them. 

## **CURSED TOUCH [2 FP]** 

_The Enemy can drain your life force with a simple touch of their hand, or by feeding on your blood._ 

Adventurers who fail to score at least a Basic Success during their Reaction Turn become Weak. If they are already Weak, they lose 1 additional Grit. 

## **DARK SENSES [2 FP]** 

_The Enemy sees you. The Enemy hears you._ 

The Adventurer can never hide from the Enemy and suffers -1 to all rolls made to surprise the Enemy. Additionally, Partial Cover offers no benefit while fighting this Enemy. 

## **DEADLY POISON [2 FP]** 

_The Enemy secretes lethal venom, or wields poisoned weapons._ Adventurers who fail to score at least a Basic Success during their Reaction Turn become Poisoned. If they are already Poisoned, they lose 1 additional Grit. 

## **ELEMENTAL FURY [1 FP]** 

_The elements answer the Enemy’s call._ 

The Enemy can control one or several elements, and use them to hinder their opponents or strike at them. Thanks to their magic, the Enemy can attack from a distance even without weapons.
