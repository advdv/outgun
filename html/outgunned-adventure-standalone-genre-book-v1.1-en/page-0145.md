# Spending Cash 

You can spend your Cash to buy new gear or services, to bribe somebody, or to pay for first-class accommodations or some medical expenses. Everything you can buy has a price between 1$ and 3$. 

- ♦ **1$:** Gear and services for everyday use or of little consequence. _A pistol, a toolbox, a couple nights in a flophouse._ 

- ♦ **2$:** More expensive or harder to find gear and services. _A saxophone, a hunting rifle, safe passage through a border._ 

- ♦ **3$:** Luxury gear and services, rare items, or specific requests. _A military radio, a machine gun, transportation for the group on a zeppelin._ 

All pieces of gear and services that cost more than 3$ cannot be bought with money and must be earned by taking on side assignments, granting favors to the right people, stealing from the bad guys, or in some other way. 

Obviously, spending your fortune is not the only way for you to lose Cash. On your adventure, you run the risk of losing Cash because of a failure that causes you to leave your wallet behind, or because you got robbed or captured by the enemy. 

# Do you take cards? 

So long as you’re near home, or you stick to the big capitals of the World, Cash is always a valuable resource that can make your adventuring experience easier. 

Unfortunately, when you venture into faraway countries that use a different currency, or where money doesn’t hold the same value, Cash can just as easily become useless. 

As such, it’s VERY important for you to get everything you need before you set off, so you won’t end up in the middle of the Amazonian jungle without toothpaste, or ammunition.
