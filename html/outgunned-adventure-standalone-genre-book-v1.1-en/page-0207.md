# The role of the Treasure 

When playing adventure, you are always searching for a Treasure, because the search for a Treasure is what makes you an Adventurer. 

The Treasure is the shared goal that pushes you and your companions to adventure, and it is also the reason why you cross paths with your Rival. 

All Adventurers who take part in an adventure are searching for **the same Treasure** , which is worth more than anything in the world to them. Until you find the Treasure, you and your companions are determined or forced to work together, facing the countless dangers and obstacles in your path. 

But you are not necessarily seeking the Treasure for the same motive. After all, there are many good reasons to search for a Treasure. 

For example: 

- ♦ **Truth:** You search for the Treasure in order to make a great discovery, or shed light on a long-forgotten truth. 

- ♦ **Wealth:** To you, the Treasure is nothing but a precious artifact that can be sold to the highest bidder. You need money, and this is your chance. 

- ♦ **Family:** You lost a relative and feel like the Treasure could help you find them, or learn about the mystery of their disappearance. 

- ♦ **Challenge:** You must find the Treasure in order to prove to the World that you were right, to earn recognition, or to teach your Rival a lesson. 

- ♦ **Dream:** You believe that the Treasure is much more than an invaluable artifact, you're convinced that its power could make your impossible dream come true. 

- ♦ **Coincidence:** You don’t really care about the Treasure, but finding it is the only way for you to reach your actual goal, whatever it may be.
