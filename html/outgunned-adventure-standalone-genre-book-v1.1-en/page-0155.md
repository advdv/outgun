`All Adventurers standing on the trapdoor must make a 2 Critical Reaction Roll` a `in Brawn+Stunt to jump away.`

In case of failure, the Adventurers grab onto the edge of the

Additionally, those who fail to score at least one Critical Suc-
cess lose one item from their hands.
