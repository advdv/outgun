# _The Mighty Warlock_ 

```
While they used to be a human like many others, the Mighty War-
lock sealed a devilish pact with the forces of evil, gaining
power beyond imagination. They have an army of cultists and emp-
ty-eyed warriors who follow them with no hesitation and seek a
legendary relic that will make them invincible.
```

Anyone standing against the Mighty Warlock must prepare to fight `their crazy minions, withstand their mighty magic, and see through their many lies.`
