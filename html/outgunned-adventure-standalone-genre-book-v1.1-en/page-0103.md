# **STUNNED** 

# **You cannot spend Luck to gain +1 or to activate your Feats.** 

_You’re seeing double, your ears are ringing, or your head is spinning like a carousel._ **You can get this Condition** when you’re exposed to a deafening or insistent noise, when you are blinded by a strong or sudden light, or when you fall or nearly fall down from a great height and vertigo gets the better of you. **You can remove this Condition** while at Camp, by using one of your Actions to do nothing at all, allowing your senses to settle down. 

# **FROZEN** 

# **-1 to all rolls that require precision and steady hands.** 

_You feel numb with cold, are shivering all over, and your teeth won't stop chattering._ **You can get this Condition** when you are exposed to harsh cold weather for too long without adequate clothes, when you take a sudden dip into freezing waters, or when you are hit by a snowy blizzard. 

**You can remove this Condition** by using one of your actions while at Camp to get warm, either close to a nice fire or under some heavy blankets. 

# **WEAK** 

# **You can't make Free Re-rolls.** 

_Your energies are fully depleted, you are gravely ill, or you’re turning green and on the verge of throwing up._ 

**You can get this Condition** when you get seasick or travel sick for any reason, when you fall ill, when you eat something spoiled or harmful, or when you are so bone tired that you can barely keep walking. 

**You can remove this Condition** after a day of complete rest and a nice warm meal. Alternatively, while at Camp, a friend can use one of their actions to cook something hearty for you with a Critical Action Roll Smooth+Heal. You can try to cook for yourself, but you always roll with -1.
