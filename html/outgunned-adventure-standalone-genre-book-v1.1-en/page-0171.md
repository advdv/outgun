# Second Turn Special Actions 

## **DETOUR** 

An obstacle blocks or destroys the road ahead. The Adventurers are forced to take a longer or more dangerous path, and the run duration increases by 1. 

## **FIRE AND BRIMSTONE** 

The location of the run scene is engulfed in flames, flooded with magma, or peppered with explosions. 

From now on, all Action Rolls made by the Adventurers are considered Dangerous. 

## **STUCK** 

The bag or clothes of an Adventurer snag at the worst possible time. 

An Adventurer who has fallen behind, or who went back to help another who fell behind, must leave behind their bag, backpack, or weapon. Otherwise, they lose 6 Grit. 

# Third Turn Special Actions 

## **TRAP** 

Too busy thinking about running, the Adventurers set off a trigger trap in their haste, and must make another Reaction Roll ® right away. 

## **CHAIN REACTION** 

The reckless actions of an over-eager Adventurer cause a dangerous chain reaction. 

When an Adventurer finds an opening, tries to make an Action Roll, and fails, their mistake makes the entire run scene a lot worse. During the next Reaction Turn, the Adventurers will have to make Extreme Reaction Rolls ® . 

## **DEAD END** 

An impassable obstacle, like an extremely high waterfall or wall of ice, blocks the only way out. 

The Adventurers have one Action Turn to try their best to hide or stop the source of danger. If they fail, they immediately lose the run.
