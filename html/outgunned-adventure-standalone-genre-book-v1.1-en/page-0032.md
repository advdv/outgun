The Heart<br><!-- End of picture text -->

_A loyal friend who always knows just what to say, an artist with an endless stream of anecdotes, or a first-rate chef who can make you forget all your problems._ 

The moment comes in the life of all Adventurers, when all seems lost, when even the most diehard feel like throwing in the towel. That’s when the Hearts do their best work, cheering up souls and uniting the group. 

Some of them play an instrument, others prepare a good meal. Some give wise advice, and others simply lead by example. They hold onto a smile in the hardest of times, and look at life and to their friends with love and trust.
