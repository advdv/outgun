# important Appointments 

Think of Heat like a clock that keeps ticking and ticking, something that reminds the Adventurers that their goal can't wait, and they have no time to lose on the way. 

On the 12 boxes of this tracker, the Director can choose to mark down **Important Appointments** , which will remain secret until their time actually arrive, and which will activate only if the Adventurers are too late for that appointment. 

Before the beginning of the Cinematic Campaign, the Director just needs to choose set levels of Heat and assign to them as many appointments. 

For example: 

**6:** _The Rival’s goons steal an important book from the library._ 

**8:** _The guardians take a stand to defend the Key._ 

**11:** _The Rival reaches the entrance of the Temple before the Adventurers._ 

If the Adventurers don’t find a way to prevent these appointments before the Heat reaches the set level, it means that they are too late. The events that the Director planned come true, and the Adventurers will have to deal with the consequences. 

# No, There's no time! 

Some Adventurers feel like they can get back to their hotel room every night, or that they can always afford to travel in business class just because they have the money. Others try to plan their every move, or try to ask relevant authorities and their contacts on the other side of the World for help to defeat the Rival. That’s no good. There’s no time for that here! 

Heat helps you convey to the Adventurers that there is simply no time to lose. They must make their decisions on the spot, must act with courage and cut the chit-chat to a minimum. When they forget this, the Heat rises!
