**Alaska:** _I see the medallion twirling in mid-air and run for it. I must grab it before it falls, no matter what!_ 

**Director:** _Your Rival also races for it at top speed, he has no intention of allowing you to get your hands on “his” medallion. It’s time for us to make an Opposed Roll, let’s see who will make it._ 

**Alaska:** _When I see the Rival get closer, I realize that there is no time to lose. I leap forward and try to snatch the medallion. I roll Brawn+Stunt, adding 1 and bringing my pool to 6 dice. I score . Critical Success._ 

**Director:** _Your rival is in top shape, forged by long years of military training. To do him justice, I will roll 7 dice. I rolled . Critical Success + Basic Success. Both of you jump for it, and you start doubting your chances…_ 

**Alaska:** _It’s not over until it’s over! I Re-roll, and my new result is… . Extreme Success!_ 

**Director:** _This means I have to Re-roll. I Re-roll and my new result is . Useless, not enough. You see your Rival gritting his teeth, almost grinning in celebration, but you extend your arm in a quick move. Both of you roll on the ground, and when you stand back up, the medallion is in your hand!_ 

# EXTENDED OPPOSED ROLL 

In most cases, an Opposed Check can be resolved in a single Turn, but you could also choose to pump up the tension of the scene by using the rules for Extended Rolls. 

**Extended Opposed Rolls** should be used sparingly, but they can also be extremely exciting when placed at just the right moment!
