# **The Adventure** 

The World out there is full of explorers in helmets leading well-funded expeditions, industrious researchers who brush grains of sand off of ruins, dastardly tomb robbers looking to get rich, and reckless tourists in search for a thrill, but you are not one of them. 

If you’ve chosen to become an Adventurer, your sight is trained on the Treasure, and there is no cursed temple and no deadly trap that can change your mind. 

The **Treasure** is what pushes your group to start your adventuring journey toward unexplored lands, and makes you clash repeatedly with a hateful **Rival** who wants to get the same Treasure for all the wrong reasons. Only you can save it from ending up in the wrong hands or becoming a tool of evil. 

An adventure always starts with searching for a Treasure that, to you Adventurers, is worth more than anything in the World. Sometimes you know what your Treasure will be even before filling in your Sheets, other times you will only discover it along the way. Sooner or later, however, adventure will come knocking on your door, and then there is no going back! 

## Shots and Cinematic Campaigns 

Your next adventure could last for a single Shot or span over an entire Cinematic Campaign. 

A **Shot** is an adventure that usually resolves in a single game Session. 

When playing a Shot, you and your friends only have a couple of hours to set off in search of the Treasure and face your Rival, using all the tools you learned about in previous pages. 

A **Cinematic Campaign** is a string of Shots connected to each other to create a complex plot that closely follows the classic structure of adventure movies. 

In upcoming pages you will find everything you need to create your next Cinematic Campaign, including a group sheet called the **Travel Journal** , as well as new useful tools like **Heat** and **Camp** .
