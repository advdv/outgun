Extended Rolls<br><!-- End of picture text -->

Chases, sports competitions, group stealth, exploration, attempts at building or repairing a ride... There are many challenges that an Adventurer can’t hope to carry out with a simple roll of the dice. For any such instances, you have Extended Rolls! 

**Extended Rolls** cover activities that last for more than a single Action or Reaction Roll, and they can either involve a single Adventurer, or the whole group. 

Usually, successfully passing in an Extended Roll can resolve a short scene or grant you huge advantages. Similarly, failing an Extended Roll is worse than getting a simple Action Roll wrong, and it carries consequences that will have a bigger impact on the story. 

The Director can call for an Extended Roll when they wish to draw out a moment or grant more gravitas to an action. 

Not all Extended Rolls are resolved the same way, there are 6 different types, described in the upcoming pages. As a Director, feel free to choose the best method of resolution for the circumstances and the experience you want to create for the scene.
