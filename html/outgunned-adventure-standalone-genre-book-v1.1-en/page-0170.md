# Run Special Actions 

The Director can also pepper the usual flow of a run scene with the occasional and annoying Special Action. 

## Other Special Actions 

In Section 6, you can find many new Special Actions that Supernatural Enemies can take during a run scene. The Director is also free to come up with their own Special Actions depending on the run scene they have in mind. 

Finally, other Outgunned books also include many Special Actions designed for Chases, which can easily be adapted for desperate run scenes on a ride. 

You can use 1-Adrenaline Special Actions during the first Turn, 2-Adrenaline Special Actions during the second, and so on. Fully compatible! 

Special Actions can make a run much more complicated and thrilling, but they are fully optional. 

The Director can activate a Special Action **once per Turn, at any moment in the Turn** . Regardless of the run duration, the Director can only activate up to 3 Special Actions. 

Up next, you find a list of Special Actions that the Director can use starting from the first, second, or third Turn of a run scene, organized by how risky they are. 

## First Turn Special Actions 

### **BAD CALL** 

It seemed as if everything was going for the best, but someone made a bad call. 

Even if all Adventurers scored at least a Basic Success, one of them misjudges their move and **falls behind** . 

### **THAT HURTS!** 

The Enemy or trap that threatened the Adventurers turns out to be even more dangerous than they looked. 

Adventurers who lose at least 2 Grit during their next Reaction Roll also become Hurt or Poisoned. 

### **BY SURPRISE** 

The Adventurers are taken by surprise. During their next Reaction Roll , they cannot make use of their Feats or Luck.
