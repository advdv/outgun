# The Key and the Clues 

# Narrating with Clues 

You have an incredible plot all planned out and you want the Adventurers to get invested in the story without dumping tons of useless information on them. I know, we’ve all been there! 

The good news is that clues are your friends. Think of clues as fragments of crucial information that the Adventurers are striving to collect and then make them work hard to find some shards. 

This way, you won’t have to worry about giving out details right away, as the Adventurers will ask you for more themselves, and work hard to uncover your wonderful story by piecing together the clues like a puzzle. 

During the Cinematic Campaign, the Treasure is like the unreachable horizon all Adventurers try to reach, always one step away but never within grasp. 

Fortunately for you and your companions, the Treasure is not everything, there are many precious breadcrumbs you can pick up along the way of your thrilling journey. 

♦ **The Key:** The Key is an item you need in order to reach the Treasure. It could be an ancient diary you need to decipher, a mysterious map, a staff with a jewel that will light your path, or maybe even an actual key to open who-knows-what who-knows-where. The Key is an excellent “small treasure” you can grant to the Adventurers after they make it through a Temple littered with traps, and it’s also the perfect excuse to sic the Rival after them. To learn more about Keys, go to **Section 4: GUNS & GEAR** . 

- ♦ **The Clues:** Along their path, the Adventurers can discover a string of clues that will help them find the Treasure, make their way past the traps that protect it, and discover truths that were kept hidden or forgotten. 

An interesting way to reward the Adventurers’ choices and successes is to give them clues about the Treasure, information that can turn into precious Help in their toughest moments. Not all that’s precious is gold!
