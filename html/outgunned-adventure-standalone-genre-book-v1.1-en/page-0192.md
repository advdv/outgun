# Supernatural Rivals 



Supernatural Rivals are a unique kind of opponent. They can be real monsters straight out of legends, or individuals who used to be human and have given up everything in order to gain a dark, unnatural power. 

Occasionally, the Supernatural Rival reveals their nature from the very beginning and becomes the focal point of the entire plot. But most often, the Supernatural Rival makes their dramatic entrance only halfway through the adventure, immediately proving their power to the Adventurers by dispatching the previous Rival with ease. 

**Supernatural Rivals** simultaneously act as normal Rivals and Supernatural Enemies. Additionally, they can rely on the following characteristics: 

- ♦ **Truly Extraordinary:** All Supernatural Rivals can rely on the **Extraordinary Individual** Strong Spot, and are fearsome opponents in battle. 

- ♦ **Full Power:** Supernatural Rivals are extremely powerful and can perform wonders that go beyond normal Supernatural Feats, like parting the waters or controlling the minds of entire crowds. 

   - When playing through a Cinematic Campaign, the Supernatural Rival takes on their full power only during the **Moment of Truth** . If you face them before the final Shot, the Rival could be much weaker, or even be unable to rely on some of their Supernatural Feats. To clarify, the examples of Supernatural Enemies you found in previous pages all represent opponents who have already reached their full potential. 

- ♦ **Weak to the Treasure:** The Treasure is always the key to defeat the Supernatural Rival. Sometimes, the Treasure is a legendary weapon that can deal a lethal blow to the Rival, or a book containing magical formulas to defeat them. However, the Supernatural Rival can never be truly defeated without the help of the Treasure.
