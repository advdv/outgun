# All in 

If after making a Re-roll or Free Re-roll you got a better result, you can still choose to go **All In** . 

Once more, roll all dice that aren’t part of a success, like you would for a normal Re-roll. If you get a better result, Lady Luck has smiled upon you. 

But if you do not get a better result, **you lose all previous successes** . It’s called going all in for a reason. 

## **GOOD** 

You re-rolled and scored 

You try going All In, re-roll and score 

You got an even better result. Nice one! 

# Re-roll! Re-roll! Re-roll! 

Re-rolling is NOT a dangerous option you should only resort to in desperate situations. Far from it! 

Re-rolls are your friends, and they greatly increase your chances of success. An Adventurer worthy of the name should Re-roll most times. An Adventurer  never settles for a meager Basic Success on their first roll! 

# Recap 

After rolling the dice: 

- ♦ Tally up any successes scored 

- ♦ IF you scored at least one success, you can **Re-roll** 

- ♦ IF you have a **Free Re-roll** , you can Re-roll even when you do not score a success on your first roll 

- ♦ IF, after making a Re-roll or Free Reroll, you got a better result, you can still choose to go **All In** 

If, by the end of a session, you notice you haven’t Re-rolled at least half of your rolls, you might be playing things too safe. Loosen up. Re-roll! 

This is NOT true about going All In. All Ins are for desperate situations and should be used sparingly.
