# _Snakes_ 

_A small window opens up in the wall and with a hiss, a snake literally jumps onto you._ 

```
The Adventurer who triggered the trap must make a Critical Reac-
tion Roll  in Nerves+Cool to maintain their composure and throw
away the snake.
```

```
In case of failure, the Adventurer falls to the ground and loses
Grit. Additionally, if they fail to score at least a Basic Suc-
cess, they look Scared.
```

# _Shock!_ 

_As soon as you take hold of the artifact, a strong electric cur_ _`-` rent courses through your body._ 

```
All Adventurers in contact with the trap must make a 2 Basic Re-
action Roll  in Brawn+Endure to withstand the shock.
```

```
In case of failure, the Adventurers are electrocuted and lose
Grit. Additionally, anyone who loses at least 1 Grit due to this
trap also looks Stunned.
```
