# The Rival’s Desire 

The Rival is driven by a dark **Desire** , something they want to achieve at all costs. It’s a crazy and reckless dream worth more than anything to them, and it’s their justification for committing all manner of atrocities. Whether right or wrong, the Rival is convinced that taking the Treasure will allow them to fulfill their Desire, thus achieving happiness or peace. 

Some Rivals covet the Treasure because they want to get rich beyond measure, or because they want to enter into the good graces of their Country or Organization. Others are less mercenary, and see the Treasure as a means to spark a revolution or war, or to achieve their long-sought vengeance. 

There are even some Rivals who are obsessed with the supernatural and believe the Treasure to be a magical weapon, or a one-way ticket to eternal life. You just can’t reason with these fanatics. 

Regardless of what the Rival’s Desire is, it is certainly self-serving or flat-out evil, and **completely irreconcilable with the Adventurers’ goals** or their moral code. 

While the Rival is big, cunning, and evil, their Desire is destined to never become reality. When the Moment of Truth comes, they too will have to deal with their own conscience and pay dearly for their dastardly deeds. 

# WHAT YOU DESERVE 

A great way to give the Rival what’s coming to them is forcing them to face the ultimate consequences of their dark Desire as it consumes them (metaphorically or literally). 

Those who were trying to get rich will be buried 

under a mountain of gold, or forever sealed in the Treasure chamber. Those who sought power are forced to reckon with their own weakness, and those who yearned for immortality meet an untimely end. The universe does love its irony!
