Supernatural Enemies<br><!-- End of picture text -->

There are many stories going around. 

Travelers who return from Africa speaking of immortal warriors and shamans who can control the raging elements. Drunken sailors with horror in their voices as they tell stories about a crew of undead, sailing the sea on a ghost ship searching for a forgotten treasure. 

A French lodge of alchemists who allegedly discovered the secret to eternal life, and rumors about an abominable hairy creature as tall as two men spreading throughout Nepal. 

Some claim that an ancient emperor of China was struck by a curse that turned his entire army into terracotta statues. 

Soldiers who were lost in the Egyptian desert returned with a thousand-yearold prophecy about a mummy. 

Apparently, the world is full of **Supernatural Enemies** , and you alone can fight them. 

# Characteristics of a Supernatural Enemy 



A Supernatural Enemy has all the same characteristics of a common Enemy (see **Section 3: DANGERS & ENEMIES** ), but they can also rely on: 

- ♦ **Supernatural Feats:** Monstrous skills or features that make them especially fearsome. 

- ♦ **Bad Boxes:** 3 special Grit boxes that activate Special Actions. 

- ♦ **Special Actions:** Dangerous moves that automatically trigger when the Enemy fills in one of their Bad Boxes. 

- ♦ **Run:** Six unique actions that the Enemy can take during a run. 

- ♦ **Minions:** Loyal allies that share one of the Enemy’s Feats. 

Up next, you will find everything you need to know about Supernatural Enemies, including a list of Feats and Special Actions. And in upcoming pages, 4 readymade Supernatural Enemies, as well as their evil Minions.
