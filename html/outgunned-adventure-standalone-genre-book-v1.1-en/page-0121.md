- ♦ **Success Threshold:** The Director secretly determines a threshold, which is the number of Successes of a certain level that the Adventurers need to collect in order to pass the roll. The Adventurers can take as many Turns as they wish, trying to collect the Successes they need, but they must hurry to overcome the test. 

This method is perfect for those situations when the Adventurers are exposed to danger, or racing against time. 

**Director:** _The boulder that stands in your way is extremely heavy, it won’t be easy to move. But you will be exposed to enemy fire until you manage to make your way past it._ 

**Jason:** _I try to push the boulder away with all my strength. I roll Brawn+Force and get 2 Basic Successes._ 

**Director:** _The boulder rolls a few inches. You did move it, but it’s still not enough. Meanwhile, the enemies keep shooting at you. Get ready for a Reaction Turn._ 

- ♦ **Timed Success Threshold:** The Director secretly establishes a number of Successes of a certain level needed to pass the roll, as well as the number of Turns the Adventurers have to make their attempt. If the Adventurers haven’t gathered enough Successes when the time runs out, they have failed the roll. 

This variant is designed for those desperate situations when the Adventurers are on a clock, or when they must intervene before it’s too late. 

**June:** _As I look at the ceiling slowly lowering above me, I try to keep calm and tamper with the trap mechanism. I roll Nerves+ Fix and get a Critical Success._ 

**Director:** _You feel the mechanism responding to your lockpicks. You’re clearly on the right track. A couple more Successes should do it… but the ceiling is getting closer._
