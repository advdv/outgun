

The trap triggers (almost) every time 

A trap is like a promise, once it’s been triggered, it must go off, and it must cause some damage. 

Sure, the Adventurers can find a genius or unexpected ploy to avoid the trap, and you should ALWAYS respect and reward them accordingly. On the other hand, what kind of adventure would this be if the traps never triggered? 

I mean, once there’s a nice trap in place, that trap is bound to go off at some point. Maybe 

it will go off all of a sudden, maybe it was defective and it will only hit the last Adventurer to pass through, or maybe it will be the Enemy who triggers it. One way or another, a trap is a promise, and promises must be kept!
