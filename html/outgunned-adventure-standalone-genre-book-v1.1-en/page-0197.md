



# The Treasure 

Unique, shiny, and shrouded in mystery. The Treasure is the focal point of every adventure. 

It is the driving force that unites the Adventurers and pushes them to brave the most dangerous journey of their lives. But it is also the object of desire of a fearsome Rival who will stop at nothing in order to reach their shady goals. 

When playing adventure, you are always looking for a Treasure, but it doesn’t really matter if you will manage to bring it home. In upcoming pages, you will find some interesting cues to help you choose the perfect Treasure for your adventure. 

# We're in this together 

The Adventurers are searching for the same Treasure, but they don’t necessarily share the same motive. Maybe one of you wants to put their name on a historical discovery, while another is looking for a lost friend. 

# The Rival 

The Rival is cunning, well-prepared, and resourceful. If the two of you could join forces in search of the Treasure, the adventure would be a walk in the park. Unfortunately, they are an absolute jerk with lines like “I will make the World pay!” You can’t reason with them. 

Each adventure comes with an evil Rival who is trying to get their hands on the Treasure for all the worst reasons, and you will have to stop them, come what may. 

In upcoming pages, you’ll find tools and tips to set the stage for a memorable Rival. 

Regardless of their personal reasons, all Adventurers are working together to search for the same Treasure, and the circumstances are forcing them to cooperate and trust 

each other. Only together can you succeed!
