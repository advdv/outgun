> RESOURCES OF AN ADVENTURER ~~a~~ Aside from Attributes and Skills, you can also rely on several resources that allow you to get important advantages, to keep track of your condition, and even to cheat death when all seems lost. 

When creating an Adventurer, you start the game with all your **Grit** and no **Conditions** . Trust me, that won’t last! 

Additionally, you start off with **1 Luck, 1 Spotlight, 1 Cash** , some pieces of **Gear** , and top it all off with **1 Lethal Bullet** in your **Death Roulette** . 

# Grit & Conditions 

Your Grit represents your ability to shake off wounds and stress. It’s what allows you to keep standing back up after every failure. 

On your Adventurer Sheet, you’ll find 12 empty boxes that represent your Grit. Whenever you lose some Grit, you must fill in some of those boxes. 

Then there’s the You Look section of your Adventurer Sheet, which allows you to keep track of Conditions, which are the consequences of bad injuries, exhaustion, and other situations. 

We will discuss Grit and Conditions in depth in **Section 3: DANGERS & ENEMIES** .

Spotlights are an even more precious resource, that allows you to get everyone’s attention by accomplishing feats worthy of a character in an adventure movie. You begin the game with 1 Spotlight, and can spend it to gain a success without rolling dice in many circumstances, to remove a Condition, or to save one of your friends when they're about to meet their bitter end. 

We’ll go in depth about Luck and Spotlight later, in **Section 2: BASICS OF THE GAME** .
