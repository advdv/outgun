## _Run Actions_ 

`1. The Mummy creates a terrible sandstorm.` **`Roll Brawn+Endure.`** 

`2. The Mummy hurls you against a wall of sand.` **`Roll Brawn+Survival.`** 

`3. A swarm of locusts emerges from the Mummy’s mouth.` **`Roll Brawn+Fight.`** 

`4. The Mummy calls on the sand to swallow you.` **`Roll Brawn+Dexterity.`** 

`5. The Mummy unnaturally unhinges his jaw, his howls echo in the wind.` **`Roll Nerves+Cool.`** 

`6.` The Mummy unleashes a rain of fire. Seek shelter. **`Roll Nerves+Detect.`**
