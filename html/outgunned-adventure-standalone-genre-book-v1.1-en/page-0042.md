The Smuggler<br><!-- End of picture text -->

_A courier trying to evade the law, an archeologist with a mountain of debts, or an adventurer who fell off the wagon._ 

Smugglers are resourceful Adventurers who can rely on a sprawling web of contacts and always know how to find a way to safely cross any  borders. 

Most Smugglers are crooks and troublemakers who help their friends evade controls by corrupt law enforcement agencies. Others are grave robbers getting a long-overdue chance at redemption. But all of them are much more than what catches the eye, they know the story and the Treasure better than anybody else. 

**Background:** 

Archeologist, Grave Robber, Pilot 

**Catchphrase: Flaw:** Who d’you think you're talking to? I need money They don’t need it anymore I have unfinished business Either I do this, or somebody else will I don’t believe in my friends
