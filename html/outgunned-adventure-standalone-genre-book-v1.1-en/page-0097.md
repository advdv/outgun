# The consequences 

A roll with multiple difficulty could come with a single possible consequence, worse than usual, or with two completely separate consequences. The Director is tasked with clearly establishing the consequences of the roll. 

**Director:** _Getting onto the branch requires a Critical Success, but to do so before the monkey completely empties your backpack you also need a Basic Success. The situation requires a Critical + Basic Roll. Good luck!_ 

# Failure 

When facing a multiple difficulty roll, if you do not score even one of the successes you need, it means you failed across the board and must face all consequences. 

**Jason:** _Nothing, not even one tiny success!_ 

**Director:** _The monkey starts pulling things out of your backpack and as it rummages you see your trusty compass falling into the thick vegetation beneath you. That’s probably why you get distracted and lose your grip and fall down, crashing into a bigger branch and losing 3 Grit._ 

On the other hand, if you score only one of the successes you need, it means you only failed one part of the roll. 

If the roll had a single consequence, you can at least avoid failing completely. And if there was more than one consequence, you managed to reach at least one of your goals. 

**Jason:** _I rolled a good old Critical Success._ 

**Director:** _Huffing and puffing, you hoist yourself onto the branch, but that requires your full concentration. When you finally breathe a sigh of relief, you can spot the monkey at the base of the branch, slamming your compass against the wood before looking down at you, and clambering away. At least you're in one piece._
