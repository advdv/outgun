```
Greenland, 1936.
```

**1** 

```
The scene opens with the ADVENTURERS hanging on the steep wall of
a frozen mountain. They can barely glimpse the end of the climb
above them, while beneath, thick white clouds hide a terrifying
precipice. Nobody would survive such a fall.
Their only option is to keep climbing to reach their destination
with Odin’s Key.
```

`The ADVENTURERS are tied to each other with a thick rope and must` climb in single file, as ruthless winds try to yank them from `their handholds.` 

**2** 

The first in line is responsible for placing large pitons in the `frozen wall to secure the rope, creating a safe holding point for the climbers coming after them. The character who volunteers to place the pitons must make an` **`Action Roll in Brawn+Survival`** `.` 

`If they score a` **`Critical Success`** or greater, they manage to firmly `plant the piton in the ice, and the other ADVENTURERS can keep climbing without much trouble until they reach the mountain summit.` 

`If the ADVENTURER only scores a` **`Basic Success`** `the piton creates a small but dangerous crack, which could open wider as the others put their weight onto it. Every ADVENTURER who uses that piton` on their climb must flip a coin. If the result is Heads, the crack `becomes too wide and the piton suddenly slips out. Finally, if the ADVENTURER who places the piton scores` **`no Success at all`** `, the piton gives way as soon as the next person hangs onto it.` 

**3** 

```
If the piton slips away, the ADVENTURER who was using it to climb
runs the risk of falling down, as does everyone behind them [Ba-

TURERS who had already climbed over the piton, including the one
who planted it, risk being dragged down by their allies [Basic
Reaction Roll  in Brawn+Endure].
```

After overcoming this possible hiccup, the ADVENTURERS can final `- ly end their grueling climb and reach the summit. Once there, they can clearly see an imposing` **`burial structure`** `that looks like a small mausoleum nestled in the frozen landscape. Professor WIELOCH was right, the tomb of Erik the Red exists and they have found it.`
