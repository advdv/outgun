# _Ahnenerbe_ 

```
Allegedly, the Führer is obsessed with the supernatural, and
some claim that he sent agents all over the world to search for
legendary weapons, lost technologies, and divine artifacts to
help him conquer the world.
```

`The Ahnenerbe (the association for research and preservation of ancestral heritage) is a special department of the Nazi SS ded-` icated to historical, scientific, and pseudo-scientific research. 

Imagine the worst possible team of officers and scientists who `covet power and forbidden knowledge as they roam around the world in search of legendary Treasures with alleged magic powers. You can see why these maniacs are the perfect enemy for adventures that include a` **`Supernatural Threat`** `.`
