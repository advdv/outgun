# SUPERNATURAL FEATS 

A Supernatural Enemy can rely on **6 Feat Points** (FP), which the Director can use to assign unique and powerful Supernatural Feats. 

Each Supernatural Feat has an assigned cost between 1 and 3 FP, depending on its strength, and allows you to customize the Supernatural Enemy in order to offer an ever-thrilling experience. 

For example, the **Elemental Fury** Feat costs only 1 FP, and allows the Enemy to control one or more elements through their magic. The **Immortal** Feat, on the other hand, costs 3 FP and makes the opponent completely immune to bullets and fire. 

# BAD BOX AND SPECiAL ACTiONS 

When creating a Supernatural Enemy, you can choose to turn up to 3 of their Grit boxes into **BAD BOXES .** Once that’s done, their Grit track should look something like this: 

Whenever you fill in a Bad Box, the Enemy **immediately** uses one of their Special Actions. Special Actions are drastic maneuvers or secret techniques that will really put the Adventurers to the test. 

A Supernatural Enemy knows up to **4 Special Actions** , and the Director is free to choose which of those to trigger when they fill in a Bad Box. 

For example, the Special Action **To me!** Allows the Enemy to recall their Minions, while **Mind Control** allows them to take over the mind of one Adventurer. 

While it’s best to change things up, the Director can choose to repeat the same Special Action several times during the same fight.
