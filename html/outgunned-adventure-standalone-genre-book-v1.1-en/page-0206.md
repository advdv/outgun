Treasure<br><!-- End of picture text -->

What is it that you want, more than anything in the World? 

Many yearn for infinite wealth. Mountains and seas of glittering gold, of fine jewels and exquisite precious gems. Others passionately strive for glory, they envision a future where they are surrounded by admirers. 

There are some who are simply trying to find themselves, or a fragment of their sad past, and hope that the Treasure will somehow fill the void that aches in their heart. 

The most foolish chase after an unnatural dream. A source of eternal life, a power that nobody should control. 

The **Treasure** is an ancient artifact, lost and maybe even forgotten. It is oneof-a-kind and priceless, and its story is inextricably entangled with legend. It is a wonderful and unreachable horizon that draws the Adventurers to adventure, that drives them to set off on a long and thrilling journey. 

But the Treasure is also dangerous and shrouded in many layers of mystery. A precious and abandoned jewel that challenges the human race with its mere existence, and it lies hidden, waiting for the first lucky soul who finds it. It is a siren song that calls to Adventurers and Rivals, leading them to cursed temples and deadly traps, forcing them to face their darkest desires.
