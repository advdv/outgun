# Camp Actions 



While at Camp, each Adventurer can take 2 actions among: 

- ♦ **Investigate:** An Adventurer can use an action to decipher, translate, or analyze a clue, to gather information from nearby sources, or to further their research. 

   - **Raymond:** _I look for a quiet spot, far from the others, and focus on reading the manuscript, trying to find a clue or a code in its pages._ 

- ♦ **Heal:** An Adventurer can use an action to remove a Condition from either themselves or a friend. 

   - **June:** _I take the first-aid kit and march toward Alaska with an annoyed expression. “Here, I’ll do it.” I say as I pour some disinfectant on a clean dressing._ 

- ♦ **Fix:** An Adventurer can use an action to repair an object, a weapon, or a ride. **Alaska:** _This old seaplane has seen better days, but I’m sure it still has some flight in its wings. I open the engine compartment to let the smoke come out, and start working on the repairs._ 

- ♦ **Comfort:** An Adventurer can use an action to comfort their friends by playing some music (Smooth+Style), by preparing a delicious meal (Smooth+Heal), or in some other way. If the Adventurer succeeds in a Critical Action Roll, all other involved parties receive the positive Condition **Relaxed** (See Uncommon Conditions in **SECTION 3: DANGERS & ENEMIES** ). 

   - **Jason:** _We’ve all been through a lot these days. I want to light the fire and try cooking my favorite soup for them._ 

- ♦ **Resources:** An Adventurer can use an action to look for resources in the area, or to go shopping in a nearby establishment. **Raymond:** _We can make some torches to explore the cave, but first I need several wooden sticks and some resin. Maybe I can find some around…_
