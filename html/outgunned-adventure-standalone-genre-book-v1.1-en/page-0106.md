Enemies<br><!-- End of picture text -->

Whether they are street thugs or Nazi soldiers, savage beasts or Nazi secret agents, a mysterious hitman or a huge bald Nazi with a mustache, your Enemy is always ready to get in your way. 

And there must be a good reason why so many Enemies are Nazis! 

**An Enemy is an abstract entity that represents the set of opponents** currently trying to terminate you with prejudice. 

As such, every time combat begins the Adventurers will face **a single Enemy** . The muscle-bound giant walking toward you with murder in his eyes is an Enemy, but the twenty soldiers shooting at you from all sides are also an Enemy. But what if the giant and the soldiers are fighting together? That’s still just one Enemy! Just a stronger one. 

Each Enemy can rely on a very simple Sheet with three stats: **Grit** , **Attack** , and **Defense** . Basically, an Enemy is a source of Dangerous Rolls that enters the Scene to make things difficult for you until you manage to outrun them or remove all their Grit. On the right, you find a list of Enemies ready to give you a hard time, but in upcoming pages you will also find the tools to create your own custom Enemies, and to face 

them in **combat** .
