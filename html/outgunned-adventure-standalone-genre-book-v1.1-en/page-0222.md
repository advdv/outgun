Heat<br><!-- End of picture text -->

As you get closer to the Treasure, you can feel the Rival breathing down your neck. Every step you take, every trap you overcome, every enemy you face brings you closer to the goal of a lifetime, and when the stakes are this high, the heat is bound to skyrocket. No wonder if all Adventurers are always covered in a light sheen of sweat! 

**Heat** is a dangerous clock that goes from 1 to 12, it tracks whether the Adventurers are still in the lead or if their Rival has managed to get an advantage over them. 

This tool is designed for Cinematic Campaigns, and as such holds a place of honor on the Travel Journal. 

Whenever the Adventurers fail to reach an objective, get delayed, or when the Rival gets the upper hand, the Heat rises and the Director earns some dangerous **Heat Points** , which they can use to raise the stakes and make things even more complicated. 

> If you know outgunned, you might think that you already know everything about Heat, but you’d be sorely mistaken! Keep reading, you will learn a whole new way to keep tensions high.
