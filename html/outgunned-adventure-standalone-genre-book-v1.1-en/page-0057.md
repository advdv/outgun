# **EXPLORER** 

_There is no obstacle you can’t overcome._ 

Gain a Free Re-roll when climbing, swimming, or traveling in wild environments. 

# **FAST REFLEXES** 

_You’re always alert, ready, and quick._ 

React against an Enemy by rolling Brawn+Stunt instead of the requested Skill. You ignore all -1s from Conditions and circumstances. 

# **FAVORED WEAPON** 

_Your weapon is an extension of your arm, and it never fails you._ 

Choose a melee weapon (e.g. saber, whip, boomerang, etc.). Gain a Free Re-roll when using, repairing, evaluating, or handling the chosen weapon. 

# **FIGHTER** 

_Your fists don't go unnoticed._ 

Gain a Free Re-roll for all Action and Reaction Rolls made when fighting with your hands against human enemies. 

# **FIX-IT** 

_If anyone can fix it, it’s you!_ 

Gain a Free Re-roll when repairing rides or disarming traps. 

# **GET DOWN!** 

_You grab your friend and drag them away from danger one second before disaster._ **[Full Turn]** You and another Adventurer quickly hide, dodge a hail of bullets, or avoid being run over without needing to roll the dice. 

# **GUIDE** 

_You always know where you are and where you’re going._ Gain a Free Re-roll whenever you’re finding your bearings, following tracks, locating food, or searching for your friends. 

# **GUNSLINGER** 

_The gun is an extension of your arm._ 

Gain a Free Re-roll when using, repairing, evaluating, or handling a pistol or revolver.
