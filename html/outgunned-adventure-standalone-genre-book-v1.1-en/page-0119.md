# Using an assist 

When a success on the table is what you need to tip the scales of your roll, you can use the Assist from another Adventurer to improve your result. 

For example, if you need an Extreme Success and scored 2 Critical Successes, the Assist from a friend could grant you that third Critical Success you needed to make the roll. 

When this happens, you also need to describe how your actions interact with those of your companion in order to use the Assist and achieve your success. 

**Alaska:** _I see Raymond is in trouble and run to clobber the brutes with him. I roll the dice and score 2 Basic Successes. That’s still not enough, but I can use Raymond’s Assist! I combine his Basic Success to mine and get a Critical Success. One of the shady dudes grabs the bag and since his hands are now occupied, I get a chance to punch him in the face._ 

You can only use the Assist from another Adventurer if they have made their Action Roll before you and only until the end of the Action Turn. You cannot make or use Assists during Reaction Turns. 

# What does an Assist do? 

The Assist has 3 main goals: 

- ♦ **Encouraging team play and the shared narrative.** Thanks to this tool, the Adventurers have a chance to mechanically interact with each other, and they are motivated to pay attention to the actions of their companions. 

- ♦ **Giving weight to smaller or extra successes.** Sometimes, Adventurers score lower successes or extra successes and see no creative way to make use of them. The Assist can be the perfect prompt on how to turn these small successes into great opportunities. 

- ♦ **Adding a pinch of strategy.** To get the best out of the Assist, the Adventurers must choose carefully who will roll first. Should the most prepared of them go first and leave their potential extra successes for the others; or should the Adventurer with the smaller dice pool go first, since they may not reach the target success? A pinch of strategy never hurts.
