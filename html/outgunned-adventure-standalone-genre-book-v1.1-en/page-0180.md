# List of Special Actions 



Here you’ll find a list of some Special Actions designed for Supernatural Enemies. 

As always, you are free to come up with your own Special Actions or use the ones from other outgunned books. 

When the Director fills in a **Bad Box** on the Grit track of a Supernatural Enemy, they must immediately use one of their Special Actions. 

## **BITTER END** 

The Enemy launches themselves against one of the Adventurers. 

The Adventurer is at risk of being Left for Dead, and must immediately take a spin on the Death Roulette. 

## **BURST OF MAGIC** 

The Enemy unleashes powerful magic against an Adventurer. If the Adventurer fails a 2 Critical Reaction Roll in Crime+Awareness, they become Weak. If they are already Weak, they become Broken. 

## **FRENZY** 

The Enemy is blinded by rage and can no longer feel pain. Until the end of Turn, nothing can cause the Enemy to lose Grit. 

## **FURIOUS CHARGE** 

The Enemy charges against one Adventurer with all their might. If the Adventurer fails a 2 Critical Reaction Roll in Brawn+Endure, they become Hurt. If they are already Hurt, they become Broken. 

## **MIND CONTROL** 

The Enemy tries to take control of an Adventurer, who must make an Opposed Roll in Focus+Endure. 

The Director can use 6 dice for this roll. 

If the Adventurer fails the roll, the Director takes control of their Sheet until the other Adventurers find a way to bring them back to their senses, or they run out of Grit.
