# Weapons <mark>eT</mark> 

When you set off on an adventure, you are never looking for trouble, but you should still expect it. Even though weapons aren’t always part of the standard equipment of the perfect Adventurer, a loaded gun and a nice, snappy whip never hurt anyone. You know what I mean. 

**Ranged weapons or firearms** allow you to shoot bullets and arrows at your enemies. Most of them rely on mags, which you can empty to gain +1 to a roll or to lay down covering fire. 

As a general rule, all **firearms that require two hands to be operated grant you +1 to Action Rolls made to shoot** . The same bonus does NOT apply when you are shooting two handguns at the same time. 

**Melee weapons** don't offer much in the way of advantages, unless you are trained in how to use them and can thus rely on the **Favored Weapon** Feat. In summary, unless you are a skilled sword fighter, you’re probably better off using your fists. You wouldn’t want to gouge eyes with that thing! 

In some circumstances, for example, when you’re fighting against people with sharp weapons or beasts with claws and fangs, the Director could establish that being unarmed is a Hindrance. In such cases, a melee weapon can remove that Hindrance, even if you’re not trained in its use.
