# impact of Heat 

When Heat reaches level 6, the Director earns **1 Heat Point** , which they can spend immediately to make the Adventurers’ lives more difficult, or save and wait for the perfect moment. 

The Director earns a second Heat Point when the Heat reaches level 9, and a third when the Heat climbs to 12. 

The Director can spend 1 Heat Point to cause one of these effects: 

- ♦ **Unrelenting:** The Adventurers are tired or wounded, they feel the Rival breathing down their necks. All Adventurers add a Lethal Bullet to their Death Roulette. 

   - This Heat effect is the most simple and easiest to manage, and can be a good way for the Director to heighten tensions without interfering with the plot. 

- ♦ **Tripping Hazard:** Bad luck befalls the Adventurers at the worst possible time. This Heat effect allows the Director to add an obstacle or Hindrance in the Adventurers’ path, to trigger a trap that they had managed to avoid, or to turn an Adventurer’s success in an unexpected failure. 

- ♦ **I’ll Take That:** The Rival manages to steal the Key during a Scene when they are facing the Adventurers or while they are at Camp, alternatively, they find a way to acquire another important item. The Director can also use this Heat effect to remove a Spotlight from an Adventurer. 

- ♦ **Ambush:** The Adventurers fall to an ambush and are taken completely by surprise. For the entire combat, they cannot rely on any of their Feats. 

- ♦ **Double-Cross:** Someone close to them betrays the Adventurers, or they learn that someone betrayed them at some point and they didn’t notice. 

- ♦ **I Was Expecting You:** Despite their apparent advantage, when the Adventurers reach their next objective, they will find the Rival waiting for them. How did they get there first? By all accounts, it doesn’t make sense.
