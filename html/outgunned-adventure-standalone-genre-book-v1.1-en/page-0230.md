Advancement<br><!-- End of picture text -->

Experience is not measured in years, but in miles. And you have quite a long path ahead of you, if you want to reach your Treasure. 

Even if you are already a full-fledged Adventurer, you will soon realize that you have a lot to learn before you can become the hero history needs. 

Every time you go through an **Advancement** , you gain new Feats and Skill Points that you can use to get even better in your specialties, or to expand your skill-set by exploring new talents. 

Even an old dog can sometimes learn new tricks! 

# What do i get? 

When you Advance, you gain: 

   - ♦ **2 Skill Points** to assign freely. 

- , 

   - ♦ **1 Feat** of your choice among all available, regardless of your Role and Trope. 

♦ | 

- ♦ **1 Luck** as a reward for your efforts.
