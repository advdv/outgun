# _Closing Door_ 

_On the other side of the room, a huge stone door starts to low_ _`-` er, threatening to seal the only entrance to the temple once and for all._ 

```
When the trap triggers, the Adventurers have little time to get
in (or out) before it is too late.
```

```
All Adventurers must make a Critical Action Roll to reach the
door in time.
```

```
In case of failure, the Adventurers are too late and the door
is almost sealed. In order to get on the other side at the
very last second, they must make a Critical Reaction Roll  in
Crime+Stunt.
```

# _Slipway_ 

_The Treasure is in sight, but the ground beneath your feet sud_ _`-` denly turns into a steep slipway._ 

```
When the trap triggers, the Adventurers go sliding down for a
hundred feet or more, making their way through a series of narrow
bends that take them far away from their goal.
```

```
The Director can also choose to make things more complicated by
adding Reaction Rolls  along the way.
```

```
In case of failure, the Adventurers lose Grit and can suffer the
Scared, Distracted, Stunned Conditions.
```
