# Historical Timeline 

Don't worry, you don’t need to be a History buff in order to play adventure. Just let the vintage charm of these roaring years transport you to a wondrous World where adventure lies just around the corner. 

However, if you want a general idea of what was going on in the world in the 20s and 30s, here are some interesting dates: 

**1918:** End of World War I. 

- **1920:** Prohibition begins in the United States of America, it will last until 1933. 

- **1922:** Howard Carter finds Tutankhamun’s tomb. 

**1926:** This is the year when the movie “The Mummy” is set. 

- **1929:** Wall Street crashes, starting the Great Depression. 

- **1933:** Hitler and the Nazi party rise to power in Germany. 

- **1936:** This is the year when the movie 

   - “Raiders of the Lost Ark” is set. 

- **1937:** Amelia Earhart tries her brave flight around the World.

Are you unmoved by the pulp charm of the post-war period? No worries, just grab the Outgunned corebook and dive into the amazing 90s! 

1999 is the last bulwark of a World that was modern, but not globalized. The denim-clad frayed edge before the precipice of the new millennium. An age of computers and mobile phones, where social networks and smartphones had yet to emerge. The legendary year when you could go to the cinema and see Brendan Fraser punch an ancient mummy. Ah, the nostalgia!
