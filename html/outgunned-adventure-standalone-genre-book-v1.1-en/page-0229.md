- ♦ **Explore:** An Adventurer can use an action to find their bearings in the wild or in an unknown location, to explore their surroundings to look for a path or for some tracks, or to consult a map and plot a course. 

   - **Jason:** _I’ve had enough of us roaming blindly in the jungle. While the others set up camp, I go for a walk about, trying to find a river, or another way to understand where we are._ 

- ♦ **Advice:** An Adventurer can use an action to give some advice to a friend, to train them, or to teach them something, so long as the other Adventurer is willing to use an action to listen. 

   - The Adventurer who receives the advice earns the ability to use one Feat from the friend who helped them once, this lasts until the next Camp. **Alaska:** _“I don't know if I can fly in these conditions…” I show the flight deck to June, telling her how to use the instruments to navigate “See? It’s a piece of cake!” At least for one action, she will be able to rely on my Pilot Feat._ 

- ♦ **Job:** An Adventurer can use one or more actions to do anything that requires time. For example, they can build a palisade, tidy up a room, or set a trap. 

   - **June:** _I open the backpack and take out my pencils and brushes. It won’t be perfect, but I want to make a fake copy of our map. This way, if the Rival finds us, we will have a misdirection ready._ 

In most cases, the actions taken during a Camp scene require an Action Roll, but the Director could determine that simpler tasks can be carried out automatically, without the need to roll the dice. 

When all Adventurers have taken 2 actions, or passed on their chance to do it, the Camp is over, and the group moves to the next Scene. 

The Adventurers can also agree to extend their time at Camp. If they choose to do so, they each gain an additional action, but the **Heat** rises by 1.
