2) **Deadly Traps** that will keep going until you and all other Adventurers are no more. Think : When these traps go off, they set off a slow chain-reaction of a room flooding with seawater, of a huge rolling boulder, or a ceiling that gets lower and lower to squash you flat. 

In most cases, deadly traps can be solved with an **Extended Roll** or a **Run** scene (see next section), and they will not stop until you find a way to disarm or outrun them. 

**Director:** _As soon as you turn the statue, the floor starts to shake. Entire sections of the stone floor start falling into a bottomless pit, and you realize that you must start running if you don’t want to end up just like them._

# The role of traps 

Traps are an absolute staple of the Adventure genre, you can’t go without them. Their first role in the story is usually to keep Adventurers on their toes, to thrill and surprise them at every turn. 

When things feel too quiet, you can put a trap into play to instantly heighten the tension. For example, if the Adventurers are feeling too cocky, a nice trap could be the kick in the pants they need to get in line. 

But traps are also an excellent way to slow down their exploration through an ancient or dangerous building, and to ensure that the Adventurers take the time to enjoy their journey, without missing any detail. 

Finally, traps are the perfect way to wear the Adventurers down, or take away some of their Grit. For example, before a combat begins, or as one last hurdle before they can make their well-deserved Camp.
