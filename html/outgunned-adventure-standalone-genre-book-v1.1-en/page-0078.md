# Extra Actions 



Whenever you score extra successes, you can spend them to take extra actions such as: 

- ♦ **EXTRA BASIC SUCCESS: Quick Action.** Grab or throw an item, reload a weapon, reach Partial Cover, do something quick and easy. 

- ♦ **EXTRA CRITICAL SUCCESS: Full Action.** 

   - Break through a door, get behind Total Cover, find a clue, do something that requires moderate effort. 

- ♦ **EXTRA EXTREME SUCCESS: Cool Action.** 

Jump out of a crumbling temple without getting squashed, shoot at a tiny target while running, jam the mechanism of a trap that is flooding the room, do something unbelievably difficult. 

Feats that require quick or full actions can also be activated using extra successes.
