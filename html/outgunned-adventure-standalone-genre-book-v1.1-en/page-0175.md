

# it’s all true! 

So, you believe that every legend hides a kernel of truth. I like your style! The best way to add some supernatural aspect is to choose one or several (or even all) of these elements that challenge the laws of nature. 

- ♦ **Supernatural Location:** The place where your 

   - story will take place is permeated by magical powers. Here, uncanny things can happen. 

- ♦ **Supernatural Enemies:** The opponents who stand against the Adventurers are monsters, walking corpses, or similar creatures that belong to legends. 

- ♦ **Supernatural Rival:** The Rival is no common human being, they are a mythological creature who is trying to seize the Treasure, or take over the World. 

- ♦ **Supernatural Treasure:** The Treasure hides magical or divine powers, and it cannot be allowed to fall into the wrong hands. If there’s a Supernatural Rival, the Treasure is the only thing that can stop them. 

Only you can choose where the supernatural ends and the scientifically plausible begins. A person who’s been around for 600 years due to a pact with some dark force is clearly supernatural, but what if their long life is the result of an unknown virus? Everything is possible. 

Do you want to make use of the rules for Supernatural Enemies to represent cases that border on science-fiction? That’s just fine. 

# Not like you expected 

Even if all the legends and myths from the past hold a kernel of truth, things could be different from what you have always pictured. The best way to bring supernatural elements on the scene 

in new and thrilling ways is to give the Adventurers exactly what they want, but not like they expect it. 

If you mention El Dorado, everyone will instantly picture the famous city of solid gold. But what if El Dorado was actually a man with extraordinary powers? They won’t see that coming! 

Are you planning to introduce aliens at the end of your adventure? I don’t know, the audience is still divided.
