# Luck 



You take a step into the temple and a row of spears juts out of the walls. You set one foot wrong and the floor crumbles beneath you. You read from a book and awaken an unknown curse. It’s no surprise if all unlucky Adventurers end up as hat-wearing skeletons covered in cobwebs and poisoned darts! 

Sure, being brave helps, but if you want to get back home in one piece, first and foremost, you need **Luck** . 

When you get a success against all odds, when you make a great sacrifice, when you evoke a strong emotional response from the group, or when you’re at the center of an epic and captivating scene, the Director can reward you with **Luck ( )** , up to 6 points. 

You can spend your Luck to gain several advantages: 

- : Spend 1 Luck gain +1 to an Action or Reaction Roll. : Spend 1 Luck to activate the special ability of some Feats. : Spend 6 Luck to immediately gain a Spotlight. 

## Gaining Luck 

Adventurers begin the game with 1 Luck and automatically gain 2 Luck when they fill in the Hot Box on their Grit track. 

Additionally, the Director can award an Adventurer 1 Luck whenever: 

- ♦ They get the success they needed against all odds 

- ♦ They get a far greater success than they needed 

- ♦ They anticipate a trap or solve a complex enigma 

- ♦ They learn or understand something new 

- ♦ They show great courage or are successfully reckless 

- ♦ They respect other cultures against personal interests 

- ♦ They’re at the center of an epic scene 

- ♦ Everyone cheers 

Luck is an important resource you should use very often. If you get to the end of a Session without giving out a handful of Luck, it means you’re being too stingy.
