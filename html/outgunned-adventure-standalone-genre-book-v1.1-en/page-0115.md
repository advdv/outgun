

# Extra Reactions 

During combat, you can use extra successes scored in your Reaction Turn to: 

**Take Extra Actions:** During Reaction Turns you can also take normal extra actions, for example diving behind cover or reloading a gun. See **Extra Actions** in **Section 2: BASICS OF THE GAME** . 

**June:** _I use my first success to dodge the blow, and with my extra success I toss the bag toward Raymond. “Professor! Catch!”_ 

**Counterattack:** If you score an extra success equal to the Enemy Defense, you can use it to counterattack. When you counter, the Enemy loses 1 Grit as they would when you attack. 

For example, if you make a Reaction Roll against an Enemy with Critical Attack and Defense and score 2 Critical Successes, you can use one of those to defend yourself and the other to counter, dealing 1 Grit to the Enemy. 

If you have the needed successes, you can even counter several times in one Turn. 

**Jason:** _I use my first success to dodge the blow, and with my extra success I punch the Enemy in the stomach. That’s how we do it around here!_ 

**Protect:** If you score an extra success equal to the Enemy Attack, you can also use it to protect a friend who failed their roll. 

For example, if you and another Adventurer both make a Reaction Roll against an Enemy with Critical Attack, and you score 2 Critical Successes, you can use one of those to defend yourself and the other to ensure that your friend succeeds on their roll. 

But remember, you can NOT use lower successes to do Damage Control on behalf of someone else. 

**Raymond:** _I use my first success to dodge the blow, and with my extra success I pull back Jason just a second before he gets punched. I stare at him for a moment, but I can’t come up with a witty joke to mark the occasion. Pity._
