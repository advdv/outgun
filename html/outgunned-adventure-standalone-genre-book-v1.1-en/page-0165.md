# _Collapsing Floor_ 

_It’s no surprise by now, when everything around you starts to shake, something bad is about to happen. Suddenly, a tile falls into the void. Then another. And another. You can either sprout wings, or start running!_ 

The floor begins to collapse, setting off a Run scene that lasts `3 Turns.` 

`Every turn, the Director asks the Adventurers to make a Reaction Roll` with increasing difficulty (first Critical, then 2 Criti `-` cal, and finally Extreme). For example: 



`1. Every step could be your last.` **`Roll Brawn+Stunt.`** 

`2. You must keep your balance as you run.` **`Roll Crime+Stealth.`** 

`3. The exhaustion is about to take you down.` **`Roll Brawn+Endure.`** 

`4. Suddenly, the tile you were standing on crumbles.` 

   - **`Roll Nerves+Awareness.`** 

`5. You must grab onto something to avoid falling.` **`Roll Crime+Dexterity.`** 

`6. Choose your next step carefully, you don’t want to get this wrong.` **`Roll Focus+Detect.`**
