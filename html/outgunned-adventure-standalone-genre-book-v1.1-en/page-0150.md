Whoever hid the Treasure was clearly trying to prevent others from reaching it, and they spared no expense to make sure that every corner of the temple was littered with devilish traps of all kinds. 

These people literally spent their lives designing all manners of deathly contraptions, and then built even more devious traps, in the reasonable-enough hope that nobody would survive them. Wondrous! 

# Triggering a Trap 

When you set foot on the wrong tile or touch the wrong thing with your shoddy hands worthy of a nosy Adventurer, you always run the risk of triggering a trap. 



Whenever a trap goes off, it either directly or indirectly causes Reaction Rolls (see Dangerous Rolls in **Section 3: DANGERS & ENEMIES** ). 

As a consequence, whenever you find yourself in the path of a trap you will lose some Grit and then be on your merry way. Clearly, people like you won’t give up the ghost at their first encounter with a poisoned dart, or with a room that suddenly gets flooded with molten lava. As if! 

Some traps, however, will do more than cause you to lose Grit, they will also cause additional complications. When you face one of these extremely annoying traps, you run the risk of suffering some nasty Condition, of losing pieces of your gear, or sometimes getting your whole bag or backpack snatched. 

# Avoiding a Trap 

If you realize you are standing in front of a trap, you can try to bypass it without triggering it. 

In some cases, being careful of how you move might be enough, perhaps you just need to avoid stepping on some tiles, or pulling the wrong lever. 

At other times, the Director could require you to make an Action Roll in order to work around the trap. 

Finally, sometimes the traps are tied to an enigma that you need to solve in order to prevent everything from going to ruin. 

If you manage to spot the hidden mechanism of a trap, you can even try to disarm it with an Action Roll in Crime+Fix, or with another roll of some kind depending on the trap and circumstances.
