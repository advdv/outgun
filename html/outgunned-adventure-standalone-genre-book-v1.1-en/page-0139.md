# The benefits of having a bag or backpack 

The main advantage of carrying several items in a bag is that you can take them off, pick them back up, or hand them over to a friend as a single gesture. 

Do you need to dive? You leave your bag on the shore and avoid getting your weapons wet. Do you need to toss your tools to someone else while you're hanging from a rope bridge? You take off your bag, and it’s done. 

On your adventure, time is a precious commodity, and these details can truly make a difference. 

A backpack, on the other hand, is bulkier than a bag, but it’s also more spacious and sturdier. A backpack will allow you to carry more items than what you can normally wear on you, and it could even protect them from freezing or getting wet (at least for a while). 

# Losing a Bag or Backpack 

The Director can decide that you’ve lost your bag or backpack as a consequence of your failure, or because your enemies took them from you. 

In desperate circumstances, you can even choose to sacrifice your bag or backpack to gain +1 to a pivotal roll. For example, when you are literally hanging on by a thread, and decide it’s time to leave your backpack full of weapons taken from your enemies behind.
