# Kinds of Traps 



You open the door and trigger a trap. You pull the lever and trigger a trap. You grab the treasure and trigger a trap. You get in and trigger a trap. You don’t… and still trigger a trap! 

There are hundreds of different traps scattered around the World, but all of them fall into one of three categories: 

**Trigger Traps** : These traps can be activated at a moment’s notice, as soon as you set one foot wrong, touch the wrong thing, or step in front of a light beam. Trigger traps are always instantaneous, and they were put there with the precise goal of getting rid of intruders. 

In most cases, they are fully resolved with a single **Reaction Roll** 2 , which could target only the Adventurer who triggered the trap, or it could target all nearby Adventurers. 

**Director:** _The tile sinks under the weight of your foot with a less-than-reassuring “Clank!” Before you can understand what is going on, a jet of scalding flames erupts from the wall on your left._ 

**Deterrent Traps** : These traps are not directly trying to harm you, they do wy not pose a direct threat to the safety of your group. They settle for making your life harder, in hopes that you will go no further. Imagine you are standing before a long hallway and suddenly a row of sharp blades starts to swing from side to side. That’s the warmest welcome! 

In most cases, deterrent traps are the result of a **failure** that causes a complication, and the Adventurers can choose whether to face them head-on, disarm them, or bypass them, depending on the circumstances. 

**Director:** _As you awkwardly stumble backwards you bump into a lever. All around you, you hear the sound of rusty cogs grinding into motion after many years. The armor-clad statues along the corridor all lift their weapons and take on a threatening stance. In order to proceed, you will have to make your way past these mechanical guardians._
