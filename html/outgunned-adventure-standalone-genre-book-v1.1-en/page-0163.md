# _Rolling Boulder_ 

_An ominous rumble shakes the air as a huge stone boulder bursts into the room and starts rolling after you. You can either run or get squashed!_ 

```
A rolling boulder follows the Adventurers, setting off a Run
scene that lasts 3 Turns.
```

```
Every turn, the Director asks the Adventurers to make a Critical
Reaction Roll , such as:
```

`1. The boulder keeps getting closer, you have to run.` **`Roll Brawn+Stunt`** `.` 

`2. A wall of roots and vines blocks your path.` **`Roll Brawn+Survival.`** 

`3. Small stalactites start raining from the ceiling.` **`Roll Nerves+Awareness.`** 

`4.` A cloud of bats flies past you. **`Roll Brawn+Fight.`** 

`5. The uneven ground makes it hard to run.` **`Roll Brawn+Endure`** `.` 

`6. The only way out is a tunnel covered in spiderwebs.` **`Roll Nerves+Dexterity.`** 

### 

## **`Recommended Special Actions:`** 

```
1.By Surprise 2.Stuck 3.Trap
```

```
In case of failure, the
Adventurers lose Grit and can
suffer the Distracted, Hurt, or
Tired Conditions.
```

```
If the Adventurers lose the
run, the boulder crashes into an
obstacle and leaps over them, barely
missing their heads. Then it lands,
destroying the only exit and sealing
the unfortunate souls in the tunnel.
```
