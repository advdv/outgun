# Minions 

A Supernatural Enemy can always rely on a multitude of Minions who help them to fight against the Adventurers, to keep busybodies at bay, and to carry out their evil plans. 

Some Minions are also monstrous creatures who resemble the Supernatural Enemy in some way. Others are humans who were stripped of their free will or corrupted by an evil and unnatural power. Whether willingly or not, all Minions serve their evil master, and they have no other purpose than carrying out their orders. 

Minions are common Enemies in most regards, but they can rely on one of the Supernatural Feats of the creature they have sworn fealty to. 

When coming up with a Minion, you are free to assign them any one of the Feats that also belong to the Supernatural Enemy. Bear in mind that 3 FP Supernatural Feats are extremely powerful, which makes them unfit for common minions. 

Friendly Minion Minions are evil and cowardly. But maybe not all of them! 

Now and then, the Adventurers could also find a way to earn the sympathy of a Minion who has grown tired of being ordered around, or who has found a way to partially break free 

from their master’s control. This doesn't happen often, but it’s proof that no creature is purely evil, once you learn more about them.
