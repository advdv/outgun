# **MONSTROUS CRY** 

The Enemy shouts or roars with all their might. Adventurers who fail Basic Reaction Roll in Nerves+Endure become Stunned. 

# **POWER UNLEASHED** 

The Enemy stops holding back, or draws from a power source even darker than them, and immediately gains a new Supernatural Feat chosen by the Director. 

# **PREEMPT** 

The Enemy keeps a watchful eye on their opponent and reads their next move. The next time an Adventurer states they want to make an Assist or spend 1 Luck, the Enemy butts in and preempts their move. Taken by surprise, the Adventurer loses 1 Grit. 

# **RESTRAIN** 

The Enemy uses their power, their Minions, or the surrounding environment to restrain the Adventurers. 

Adventurers who fail a Basic Reaction Roll in Brawn+Force are restrained, and suffer a Hindrance to all rolls. Adventurers can break free with a Critical Action Roll, or if another Adventurer uses their Turn to help them. 

# **SHATTER** 

The Enemy grabs an Adventurer’s weapon and shatters it into a million pieces, or melts it completely. The weapon is beyond repair. 

# **TO ME!** 

The Enemy calls their loyal Minions, and then flees. 

The Adventurers are immediately engaged in combat with the Minions. 

# **YOU DARE?!** 

After being struck by an Adventurer, the Enemy tosses them aside, traps them, or lands a powerful strike. 

During the next Action Turn, the Adventurer can only use their free Quick Action, but they cannot roll the dice.
