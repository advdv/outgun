Both ranged and melee weapons can also rely on one or more **Weapon Feats** , which will be described a little further on. 

# Mags 

When you set off on an adventure, you don’t need to keep track of every single bullet, stick to **mags** . 

For each weapon you can carry up to 3 mags (plus one in the weapon), which you can note by filling in the bullets you see in the **Weapons & Gear** box on your Adventurer Sheet. 

You don’t always use up mags when you shoot. 

There are only 4 ways to empty a mag: 

- ♦ **Failure:** When you fail a roll to shoot, you lose one mag. It means that you just kept shooting without really hitting your target, that your weapon jammed or misfired, or simply that that was the last bullet you had in the mag. 

- ♦ **Full Auto:** When you voluntarily empty a mag against your Enemies to gain +1 to the roll. If you’re holding two guns, you can even go Full Auto simultaneously with both and get +2 for your roll! 

- ♦ **Covering Fire:** You can shoot in your enemies’ general direction to keep them occupied. This way, your friends (not you) gain +1 to their next Reaction Roll. When you lay down covering fire, you use your full Turn and skip your Action Roll. 

- ♦ **Bad Luck:** As a consequence of your failure, the Director can state that one or more of your mags were lost, taken away, or that they became unusable. 

# Reload 

When you empty a mag, you'll have to reload before being able to use the weapon again. 

**To reload a weapon, you need a Quick Action.** When all your mags are gone, you must get more either by paying Cash or by stealing them from defeated Enemies. 

To keep things simple, weapons are divided in types and each type shares a type of mag. For example, you cannot reload an assault rifle with a pistol mag, but you can use a pistol mag to reload any handgun model.
