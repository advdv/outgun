## _Run Actions_ 

`1. The Warlock sends their minions against you.` **`Roll Brawn+Fight.`** 

`2. The Warlock strikes you with their elemental magic.` **`Roll Brawn+Endure.`** 

`3. The Warlock activates their hidden traps.` **`Roll Brawn+Stunt.`** 

`4. The Warlock blocks the path ahead or summons an unexpected obstacle.` **`Roll Nerves+Survival.`** 

`5. The Warlock vanishes into thin air. Then reappears right in front of you.` **`Roll Crime+Awareness.`** 

`6. The Warlock shatters the ceiling, raining stone and debris upon you.` **`Roll Nerves+Awareness.`**
