# **AN EASY SCORE** 

An old acquaintance of yours calls to pitch a nice, easy job. You just need to take someone to pick up something and then take them back. Obviously as soon as you get there, people start shooting at you, and everything goes to shit in no time flat. When you end up with the precious Key, you have no idea what it does, but the Rival knows and they are coming for you. 

# **IN THE WRONG HANDS** 

As you are traveling, a guy on the brink of death practically falls on you, giving you a mysterious Key and an even weirder mission: stop the bad guys from getting the Treasure. You don’t even have time to realize what is happening and everyone is already shooting at you. Apparently, the fate of the World is in your hands, and it’s only Wednesday! 

# **WITH YOUR OWN EYES** 

At some point of your life you have actually seen the Treasure, or the temple where it’s hidden. And now all the others who were there with you are dead. When a group of Adventurers set off in search of the Treasure, they were bound to ask for your help. After all, you are the only one who knows the way. 

# **THIS IS MY HOME** 

For centuries, your homeland stood guard over a legendary Treasure. When a ruthless Rival begins to trample over your people and destroy everything in their path, you realize that you cannot stand by and watch. You must fight for your home and prevent the Treasure from being used for evil. 

# **THAT DOES NOT BELONG IN A MUSEUM** 

In the past, you were unable to save the Treasure from the claws of the Rival, or maybe even made the unforgivable mistake of stealing the Treasure for them. Now you finally have a chance to get even. You just need to take the Treasure and bring it back to its rightful owners. 

# **LEFT WITH NO CHOICE** 

I don’t know what you did, but someone clearly took offense at that. Now they have taken hostage your loved one, gathered enough proof to lock you up for good, or maybe they have simply put a gun to your head. You have two weeks to get them the Treasure, or you are done for.
