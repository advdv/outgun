# What pushed you to adventure? 



If you’ve chosen to set off in search of the Treasure, you must have your reasons. Otherwise, why would you go this far only to visit a temple filled with deadly traps while bad guys shoot at you? 

Here you can find 10 perfect prompts for a great adventure. 

## **YOUR LEGACY** 

Your parents or grandparents entrusted you with an old family heirloom, an ancient artifact dating back to a forgotten time. This strange gift spent years accumulating dust in your home, until you found an old letter or a hidden will and learned the truth behind its creation. It is a Key that can take you to the Treasure. 

## **THE WORK OF A LIFETIME** 

You’ve spent years gathering clues about the Treasure, and after many sleepless nights, you are this close to the discovery of the century. Unfortunately, your colleagues at the museum or university don’t believe you, actually they are quite certain you've gone mad. 

But when everything seems lost, adventure comes a-knocking, and a new clue shows you the way to the Treasure. 

## **FOR YOUR COUNTRY** 

Outside of your office there are two government agents waiting for you with serious expressions. The Nazis or some other nut like them is trying to find the Treasure, and your Country needs you to stop them. Only you have what it takes to get there before the bad guys and stop them from using the Treasure to change the course of History. 

## **IN MEMORIAM** 

An old friend dedicated their life to searching for the Treasure, but in the end, they never made it back from their last expedition. 

After many years, you find their journal and start piecing together the itinerary of their journey. You firmly believe that it is now your duty to find the Treasure. After all, it’s what your friend would have wanted, and it’s the only way you can feel like they are still with you.
