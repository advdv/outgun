# **SUBTERFUGE** 

_What the others don’t know can’t hurt them. And most importantly, it can't hurt you._ Gain a Free Re-roll when lying, deceiving, or cheating. 

# **TEAMWORK** 

_Someone distracts them, and you strike. That’s what I call teamwork!_ **[Full Turn]** After another Adventurer gave you an Assist this Turn, you can spend 1 Luck to gain an automatic success without rolling the dice. 

# **THAT WAS CLOSE!** 

_Thanks to your instincts or to a stroke of luck, you move away before it’s too late._ Repeat one Reaction Roll of any kind. Ignore all -1s from Conditions and circumstances. You must keep the new result. 

# **THRILL SEEKER** 

_When things get serious, just get out of the line of fire._ 

Gain a Free Re-roll when dodging bullets, arrows, or triggered traps, and for all rolls to get out of the way and find cover. 

# **TOO YOUNG TO DIE** 

_You’re young and carefree. You feel like nothing can stop you._ When filling in the Bad Box, you suffer no Condition. 

You can only gain this Feat when creating a Young Adventurer. You cannot choose this Feat while Advancing. 

# **TRAILBLAZER** 

_Where others see obstacles, you find a path._ 

**[Full Turn]** Create a safe path, an alternative route, some space, or an opening thanks to your technical or natural knowledge. 

# **WATCH AND LEARN** 

_If they can do it, so can you._ 

After another Adventurer succeeds in an Action Roll this Turn, you automatically succeed in the same action without rolling the dice.
