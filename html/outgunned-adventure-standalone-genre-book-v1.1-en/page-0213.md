# _Book of Anubis_ 

`In the sixth tome of his Stromata, Greek philosopher Clement of Alexandria speaks of a legendary library where Egyptian priests collected all of their knowledge and secrets. From their ancient building techniques to cutting-edge medical procedures. Allegedly, one of these books was unlike all others. It was not` written by a normal priest, but by a god made of flesh and bone. 

```
The Book of Anubis is a legendary manuscript made of solid gold.
According to the myth, it was written – or at the very least
dictated – by Anubis, the jackal-headed god that watches over
necropolises and the underworld.
```

`Some believe that this text is nothing but an incredibly valuable` tome that records the secrets of mummification. But according to `those who believe in magic, the Book of Anubis is an actual grimoire enshrining forbidden spells that can open the gates of the underworld and bring the dead back to life.`
