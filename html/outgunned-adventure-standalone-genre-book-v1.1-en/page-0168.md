> Run Reaction Turn ~~SR~~ <mark>ae</mark> In most cases, run scenes are composed of consecutive Reaction Turns and the Adventurers have no choice but doing their best to survive and live to fight another day. 

At the beginning of each Turn, the Director describe the Adventurers’ situation and asks them to make a **Reaction Roll** 2 (See Reaction Rolls in **Section 3: DANGERS & ENEMIES** ). 

Need inspiration for what roll the Adventurers should make? Here is a list of examples: 

**1.** A huge boulder rolls toward you at full speed. **Roll Brawn+Stunt** 

**2.** A stalactite breaks off from the ceiling, falling on you. **Roll Crime+Awareness** 

**3.** A wall of roots and vines blocks your path. **Roll Brawn+Survival** 

**4.** The road is blocked, you must find another way. **Roll Focus+Detect** 

**5.** The corridor gets smaller and narrower. **Roll Crime+Stealth** 

**6.** The tunnel splits in two, choose quickly. **Roll Nerves+Cool** 

Most Reaction Rolls 2 made during a run scene have **Critical or 2 Critical Difficulty** , but the Director can also choose to make them harder or easier depending on the circumstances. 

I recommend you try to make all run scenes very challenging, and maybe increase the danger levels as the end grows near. Don’t be afraid to cause the Adventurers to lose too much Grit, actually, you should try to put some fear into them. Once this is all over, you can always allow them to **Catch a Break** ! 

If an Adventurer fails their roll, they lose Grit as usual, and possibly encounter additional complications, like suffering a Condition or losing some Gear. 

# Free Reactions 

Usually, the Director determines the Attribute and Skill the Adventurers need to roll for a Reaction Roll. However, during a run scene, they can also allow the Adventurers to react freely, using the characteristics that feel most appropriate to them.
