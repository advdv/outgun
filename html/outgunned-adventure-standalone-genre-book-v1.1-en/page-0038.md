The Technician<br><!-- End of picture text -->

_A demolition expert, a radio technician, a mechanic with years of experience, or a sapper in the army._ 

In a world full of reckless Adventurers who charge into the action head-first, Technicians are a rare example of pragmatism and competence. When something breaks, they are the ones who fix it, and when something is still standing, they can just as easily tear it down. When a Technician sets off to solve a problem, there is no obstacle too big, no mechanism too complex, and no tool too improvised to stop them. 

**Background:** 

Mechanic, Demolition Expert, Sapper 

**Catchphrase: Flaw:** It doesn’t work like that… I don’t take advice What did I tell you? I’m a fatalist and a pessimist Step back a little I can’t swim 

# Characteristics of a Technician 

SPECIALIZED. CAN FIX IT. PRACTICAL.
