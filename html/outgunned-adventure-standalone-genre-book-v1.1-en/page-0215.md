# The forces of the Rival 

All Rivals are ruthless but prepared individuals who can rely on the assets, means, information, and goons they need in order to steal the Treasure. If the two of you could work together, the Adventure would be a walk in the park. Unfortunately the Rival is an actual jerk that nobody would ever want anything to do with! On top of this, each Rival can also depend on 2 or 3 Strong Spots among: 

- ♦ **Private army:** The Rival is a high-ranking member of an army of some kind, who can rely on the unquestioning support of their soldiers, or has paid off an army of mercenaries. 

- ♦ **Unlimited funding:** The Rival is loaded; they can afford to buy the whole island where the Treasure is located. There is no limit to their means. 

- ♦ **Extraordinary individual:** The Villain is mighty and strong, or an expert in hand-to-hand combat, or a supernatural creature. Even alone they are a fearsome enemy. 

- ♦ **Luminary:** The Rival is gifted with the mind of a genius and knows every detail about the Treasure and the surrounding history. Their theoretical knowledge can put even a Professor to shame. 

- ♦ **Survivor:** The Rival braved extreme circumstances and wild environments and survived. And what they have learned has made them capable of surviving all situations, even without any rations or gear. 

- ♦ **Occult knowledge:** The Rival is an occultist who can rely on paranormal knowledge. 

- ♦ **Control of the territory:** The Rival earned or bought the trust of the government, of law enforcement forces, or of the natives in a set location. When the Adventurers are in the Rival’s territory, everyone is against them. 

- ♦ **Leverage on the Adventurers:** The Rival knows something about the Adventurers’ past, or they have one of their friends hostage, or can rely on another trick to keep their opponents in check. In order to stop them, that leverage must be neutralized.
