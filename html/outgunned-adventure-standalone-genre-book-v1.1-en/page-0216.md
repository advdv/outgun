# The Rival’s Weak Spot 

The Rival has one or two Weak Spots that the Adventurers can try to take advantage of in order to get an edge. 

The **Weak Spot** of a Rival could be a literal weak spot, like a bad leg that’s a reminder of an old battle. But it’s more likely to be a piece of information that can be used against the Rival, something that carries a connection to the source of their Desire. 

For example, “ _Never backs down from a challenge_ ”, “ _Afraid to disappoint his son_ ”, “ _Deaf in one ear_ ”, or “ _Betrayed her soldiers_ ”, are all perfect Weak Spots for a Rival. 

During a Cinematic Campaign, the Point of No Return could be an excellent time to give the Adventurers a chance to uncover one of the Rival’s Weak Spots. 

For example, the Adventurers could learn the Rival’s secret by eavesdropping on a conversation while infiltrating their camp, or by rifling through their personal effects, or by interrogating their right hand. 

When the Adventurers learn the Rival’s Weak Spot, they can then take advantage of it to gain **Help** for a relevant action. 

**Director:** _The Rival holds Jason by the neck, threatening to drop him from the tower at any moment. As the rain keeps relentlessly pelting down on them, a flash of lightning shatters the night, reflecting the unbridled fury in the Rival’s eyes. He looks completely beside himself._ 

**Alaska:** _I know his Weak Spot, he is doing all this in order to save his daughter and wouldn’t want to disappoint her. I look him straight in the eye, feeling the determination rush through my body, and shout “Stop! Can’t you see what you’ve become… What would your daughter think?”_ 

**Director:** _The Rival’s eyes soften for an instant, his mind clearly straying toward the memory of his daughter. The distraction causes his hold on Jason to loosen, and you can all see this is your chance, it’s time to make your move. Next turn, you will have a Help!_
