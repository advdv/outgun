

# _Revolving Walls_ 

_When you touch the statue, the wall spins, dragging you to the other side. Your friends turn around, and you are nowhere to be found._ 

```
When the trap triggers, at least one Adventurer is dragged to
another room, separated from the group.
The Adventurers who fell into the trap can attempt a Critical
Action Roll (e.g. Focus+Detect) to look for the mechanism in
the wall and make an attempt to get back, but they always roll
with -1.
```

**`In case of failure`** , the Adventurers still find the mechanism and `get back to the starting point. However, they also accidentally activate a second trap in the process, dragging away the other Adventurers.`
