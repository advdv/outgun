# _The Templar Treasure_ 

```
At the beginning of the XII century, Geoffrey de Saint-Omer and
Huges de Payns founded the order of the “Poor Fellow-Soldiers
of Christ and of the Temple of Solomon”, usually known as the
Knights Templar.
```

```
In 1312, King Philip IV “the Fair” disbanded the order forev-
er and sentenced Grand Master Jacques de Molay to death. Be-
fore breathing his last, de Molay casts a terrible curse on all
his enemies.
```

`In the two centuries that separate these events, the Knights Templar took part in several crusades, quickly becoming one of the` most influential and powerful orders in history, and amassing an `unbelievable amount of wealth.` 

```
The Templar Treasure is a literal pile of gold coins, jewels, art
pieces, and precious gems collected as donations from wealthy
worshipers and as loot from the Holy Land. An amount of precious
goods that would enable a country to rise above all others and
bring the world economy to a standstill.
```

```
According to some, the Templar Treasure is hidden in a secret
room in the historical fortress in Acre. Superstitious people
believe that it was moved to a crypt beneath Paris, zealously
protected by the curse of the last Grand Master. However, most
agree that the Treasure has changed hands several times, cross-
ing the Atlantic in the hold of Christopher Columbus’ caravels,
and is currently somewhere in North America.
```
