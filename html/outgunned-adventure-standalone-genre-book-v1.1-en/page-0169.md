# Final Run 

**Fall Behind:** An Adventurer who fails to roll at least a Basic Success starts slowing down or stumbling, and gets left behind by the others. 

Adventurers who fall behind suffer a **Hindrance** on their next roll, and if none of the others come to their aid they also look **Broken** , physically or emotionally. If an Adventurer slows down to help another one who is falling behind, **both** of them suffer a Hindrance to their roll, but neither becomes Broken. **Director:** _You failed to roll even a Basic Success. You see the others grow further away, and realize you are falling behind._ 

**Find an Opening:** An Adventurer who manages to lose no Grit at all is doing great, and their advantage even grants them a precious opportunity. 

Once everyone has rolled for their Reaction Turn, Adventurers who found an opening can make an **Action Roll** of their choice, something that could possibly cause the run to end before expected, or grant a precious Help to a friend in need, or make their next Reaction Turn easier. 

Once all Adventurers who earned the right to an Action Roll have made their move, the run resumes as usual with a Reaction Turn. **Director:** _Nice one! You dodge the danger and also gain some momentum. You found your opening and can act, make it count._ 

You defeated the Rival and saved the Treasure, and now everything is literally crumbling around you. As you start racing toward the light outside, a thousand traps lunge for you, and the whole temple you were just in seems hellbent on becoming your tomb. Run, damnit. Run! 

Not all Shots or Cinematic Campaigns need to end with an epic brawl on the edge of an erupting volcano. A nice, breakneck race for your life can also be the perfect conclusion to your adventure. 

Once everything is done, you can look at your friends with a sigh of relief and turn to look at the destruction that’s left in your wake. I’m certain you didn’t intend to destroy everything.
