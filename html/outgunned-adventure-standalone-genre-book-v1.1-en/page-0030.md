The Hunter<br><!-- End of picture text -->

_An explorer of the wilderness, a slayer of dangerous beasts, an expert guide, or a bounty hunter._ 

Quiet, watchful, and patient. The Hunters are always one step ahead of their prey, waiting for the right moment to strike. Some of them carry mighty bows that never miss, but most prefer a good old rifle. The one thing they all share is their deep reverence and fear toward Nature and the wild. They never forget their place in the World.

Saved by Natives, Poacher, Military Veteran
