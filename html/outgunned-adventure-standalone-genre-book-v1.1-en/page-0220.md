# _The Perfect Enemy_ 

```
The classic Rival of an Adventurer is a ruthless individual who
is looking for an ancient Treasure but holds no respect for its
history or what it can represent for its own people. The perfect
embodiment of such unending and ruthless greed are Nazis, the
violent and far-reaching arm of the Third Reich.
```

```
Those wicked cowards have no qualms burning piles of books in
the public square in their attempt to squash other cultures and
ensure the supremacy of their perverted and unfounded ideology.
```

```
To be worthy of the title, a researcher or an Adventurer must
always look at history with an open mind and an open heart, ana-
lyzing the data at their disposal with no prejudice or bias. That
is the only way for them to make great discoveries that change
the course of history.
```

```
Unlike the Nazis who, since the very beginning, have tried to
re-write history by manipulating the data at their disposal to
validate truths that align with their agenda.
```

```
And it’s exactly their eagerness to exploit history to their
advantage, and their habit of appropriating cultural heritage
from different people, that make Nazis the perfect enemy to stand
against you in Adventure.
```

```
That, and the fact that they are huge assholes who deserve to be
taught a lesson.
```
