# **A New Adventure** 

_It’s been a while._ 

_The world out there is full of unexplored jungles, lost pyramids, and cursed temples that are just waiting for an unprepared explorer to slip up. Collapsing floors, creaky bridges, poisoned darts, fire jets, and rolling boulders… You can expect all_ 

_that, and even more._ 

_But if I know you, and I’ve seen my fair share of people like you, a few obstacles and a handful of deadly traps won’t be enough to stop you. Of course not, you have your eyes set on the treasure. Wherever this journey may lead you, you will rise to the challenge._ 

_So, get up now, on your feet. Pack your bag and grab your hat. Gather your maps, and take your pistol too, for good measure. There’s no turning back now._ 

_Adventure is back._ 

## Pulp Adventure Genre Book 

adventure<sup>is a pulp adventure game inspired by the classics of the genre, from</sup> the immortal Indiana Jones, to The Mummy (1999). In adventure, the Players take on the role of brave Adventurers in search of an invaluable treasure. 

adventure<sup>is the first Genre Book in the</sup> outgunned<sup>line, and</sup><sup>**you can play it**</sup> **either standalone or as an expansion to** outgunned. 

## Director’s Cut 

adventure<sup>uses a game system called Director’s Cut, a set of mechanics using</sup> small pools of six-sided dice to determine the outcome of the Adventurers’ actions. When rolling dice, your goal is not to score the highest or lowest possible number, but to roll two-, three-, and four-of-a-kind.
