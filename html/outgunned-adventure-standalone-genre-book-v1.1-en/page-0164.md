# _Sea of Fire_ 

_The floor is lava. Literally. The only way out is to jump over some rocky islets that float over the magma, but those won’t last much longer._ 

The room floods with scalding lava, setting off a **`Run`** `scene that lasts 5 Turns.` 

```
Every turn, the Director asks the Adventurers to make a Critical
Reaction Roll , such as:
```

`1. The next safe location is far away, you’ll have to jump.` **`Roll Brawn+Force.`** 

`2. A wave of magma starts melting your shoes.` **`Roll Nerves+Cool.`** 

`3. You must run across a narrow and unstable walkway.` **`Roll Crime+Stealth.`** 

`4. The heat is nearly unbearable.` **`Roll Brawn+Endure.`** 

`5. You must wait to jump at the last second.` **`Roll Focus+Cool.`** 

`6. You must choose your next step quickly.` **`Roll Nerves+Detect.`** 

### 

## **`Recommended Special Actions:`** 

**`1.`** `That Hurts!` **`2.`** `Fire and Brimstone` **`3.`** `Chain Reaction` 

```
In case of failure, the Adventurers lose Grit, they can also lose
their bag, backpack, or some pieces of Gear.
```

`If the Adventurers lose the run, they end up huddling on the last` floating piece of rock, surrounded by a sea of fire. They will be `saved by the Rival, or by another group of unfriendly individuals who promptly capture them.`
