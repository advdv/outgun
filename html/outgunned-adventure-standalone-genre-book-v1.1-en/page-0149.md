# Who built the temple? 

When you set the scene for a nice temple, the first thing you should ask yourself is: who built this? And why? After all, if someone went to the trouble of hiding something from the whole World, they must have had a very good reason! 

Most temples were designed so that only a limited number of people have the knowledge and ability to find them, and reach the Treasure they enshrine. 

These temples are littered with traps, but they also contain riddles that can allow you to avoid them. The more you know about the Treasure and the people who hid it, the easier it will be to survive. 

Some temples were designed to test those who enter to search for a truly extraordinary individual, for example, someone with a pure heart, that kind of thing. There, you can expect to face actual challenges aimed at proving that you are worthy of reaching your Treasure. 

Finally, there are temples that were designed with the sole objective of scaring off or eliminating those who try anything funny. There, you won’t find challenges, clues, or riddles, only traps with varying degrees of deadliness. But once you’ve made it here, what are you supposed to do, head back? 

# The right time for a Temple 

If you want to get the most out of these crumbling walls, these are the 3 perfect times to place a temple in your adventure: 

- ♦ **At the beginning:** The story begins in the thick of the action, the Adventurers are standing before the entrance to a mysterious temple. 

- ♦ **Halfway through:** The temple hides a precious Key, an artifact the Adventurers need in order to set off in search of the true Treasure. 

- ♦ **At the end:** The adventure ends in a legendary temple. Deep within those 

halls, lies the Treasure the Adventurers have been looking for. Then something goes awry and everything crumbles. A classic!
