# _Rotating Bridge_ 

_The stone walkway that stretches over the chasm appears solid enough, but before you even set foot on it, it starts to spin quickly on its axis._ 

```
When this trap triggers, the bridge or walkway starts to rotate,
making it especially hard to move across.
```

```
To jump onto the bridge at the right moment, the Adventurers
must make a Basic Action Roll (e.g. Brawn+Stunt). Once on the
rotating bridge, they realize that making it to the other side
is going to be even harder, requiring a Critical Reaction Roll
 in Nerves+Endure.
```

```
In case of failure, the Adventurers land awkwardly on the bridge
and spin so much that they end up looking Weak. Those who fail
the second roll run the risk of falling down, but grab on at the
very last second and lose Grit.
```
