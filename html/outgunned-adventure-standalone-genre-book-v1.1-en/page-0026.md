The Guardian<br><!-- End of picture text -->

_A strapping adventurer who takes care of their companions, the sworn protector of a dangerous treasure, or a trusted bodyguard._ 

Guardians are strong and resolute Adventurers who strive to protect the Treasure or their traveling companions. Some of them are members of an ancient order that has been standing guard over a mysterious location or dangerous artifact. Others are simply kind souls who never hesitate to risk their own safety in order to protect their friends and even strangers. Whoever they are, Guardians are Adventurers of sound principles and you can always rely on them in times of trouble.
