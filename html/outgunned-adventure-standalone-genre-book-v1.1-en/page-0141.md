# Losing the Key 

In most circumstances it’s impossible for you to lose the Key, as the adventure couldn’t proceed without it. However, there are 4 set circumstances when the Director can choose to take the Key from you: 

- ♦ **When you are captured** , or worse Left for Dead. 

- ♦ **When it's taken** from you by the Rival or their right hand. 

- ♦ **When the Heat rises** and the Director chooses to spend 1 Heat point. 

- ♦ **When you reach the Point of No Return** , or sometimes during the Moment of Truth. 

To learn more about the Rival, Point of No Return, and Heat, go to **Section 7: TO ADVENTURE!**
