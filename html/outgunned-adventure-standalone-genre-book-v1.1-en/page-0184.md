# _The Pharaoh’s Mummy_ 

A pharaoh from ancient Egypt was mummified alive. A powerful and `terrible curse was cast on him.` 

```
So long as nobody disrupts his slumber, the Mummy shall remain
sealed in his prison in the desert. But if anyone were to awaken
him, he shall return and walk among the living to search for his ven-
geance. He shall carry with him the power to control the sands and
the plagues of Egypt, and the World will be powerless to stop him.
```
