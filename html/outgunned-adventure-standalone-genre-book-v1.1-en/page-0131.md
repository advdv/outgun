



# Getting Gear 

When filling in your Adventurer Sheet, you get some starting gear that is granted to you by your **Role** . For example, as a Daredevil, you immediately get a pistol, a rope, and a knife. If you’re a Captain, you get a compass, a pistol, and even an old ride, your old companion of many adventures past. 

Along the journey of your adventure, you can obtain new gear by borrowing it from your Enemies, or possibly by spending some of your hard-earned Cash. 

# Losing Gear 

You can lose gear as a consequence of your failure, or when you choose to sacrifice it to gain +1 to a roll that you think is extremely important. 

For example, when you squeeze through a narrow tunnel to avoid pursuit and fail, the Director could say that your bag got snagged on a root. Alternatively, when you’re treating the wounds of a friend, you can always choose to tear away a sleeve of your elegant jacket to use it as bandages and gain +1 to your Heal roll. 

Some items are so precious or important to your adventure, that you can only lose them or sacrifice them under exceptional circumstances. To learn more about such **Key Items** , just read through the end of this section. 

# Everyday Objects 

All items that play a role in your everyday life, like a hat, a diary, a deck of cards, or a pencil, are considered everyday objects. 

These pieces of gear are not always a Help or Hindrance, and can most likely be acquired without spending any Cash, but nevertheless, you should not underestimate them. 

A paper-clip or an umbrella can prove surprisingly useful under the most diverse circumstances. Think of a pen, which can be mightier than a sword!
