# **REASSURE** 

_You lock eyes with your friend, and let them know you believe in them._ Grant +1 to another Adventurer who is about to roll 4 dice or fewer, or allow a friend who has filled in their Bad Box to avoid suffering a Condition. 

# **SADDLE UP** 

_You grab your friend and start running without looking back._ 

**[Quick Action]** You and one other Adventurer skip the next Reaction Turn while running. 

# **SAILOR** 

_You have salt water in your veins._ 

Gain a Free Re-roll when driving, repairing, or evaluating a nautical ride. 

# **SENSIBLE PACKER** 

_Not even you know what’s in your backpack, but you're sure that there’s something useful._ 

Your Bag and Backpack never become a Hindrance to you, regardless of the circumstances. 

**[Quick Action]** So long as you are carrying your Bag or Backpack, you can spend 1 Luck to find a tool or weapon in it. The item found can’t be worth more than 2$, is not in sellable condition, and becomes unserviceable after the end of this Scene. 

# **SILVER TONGUE** 

_Your words speak louder than actions, and you certainly know how to talk your way out of trouble._ 

Gain a Free Re-roll for all rolls made to lie, to persuade others, to find a compromise, or to bargain. 

# **SKULKER** 

_Your prey stands no chance._ 

Gain a Free Re-roll when hiding or sneaking in wild environments. Additionally, you ignore all drawbacks from Partial Cover when you’re in the wilds. 

# **SPECIALIST** 

_No worry: this is your specialty._ 

Choose one technical specialty (e.g.: demolition, radio, excavation, etc.). Gain a Free Re-roll for all rolls regarding your chosen specialty.
