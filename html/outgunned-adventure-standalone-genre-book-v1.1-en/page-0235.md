# Using Experiences 

Experiences are like small custom-made Feats that can be called into play by either yourself or the Director in certain circumstances, and they can grant you **Help** or impose **Hindrance** at some point on your adventure (See **Help & Hindrance** in **Section 2: BASICS OF THE GAME** ). 

For example, the Achievement “ _I’ve learned to put my trust in others_ ” can grant a Help to rolls made to act in tandem with a friend. On the other hand, the Scar “ _I was locked in a room full of spiders_ ” can become a Hindrance when you’re facing an arachnid. 

Usually, **Achievements** will be of Help and **Scars** will become Hindrances, but that’s not always the case. 

For example, the Achievement “ _I’ve learned to put my trust in others_ ” may be a Hindrance to rolls made to understand whether someone is lying to you or plotting against you. On the other hand, the Scar “ _I was locked in a room full of spiders_ ” can become a Help when you’re walking through a hallway crawling with insects. At least it’s not spiders! 

Finally, **Bonds** and **Reputations** can be either an advantage or disadvantage, depending on the circumstances and people involved. 

**Raymond:** _I grab the key and walk toward the abyss, as if intending to throw it away. “If you do not let them go, you will never find the treasure”. I want to make a roll in Smooth+Speech to persuade the Rival._ 

**Director:** _Your Achievement “I overcame my fear of heights” works in your favor. You have no qualms getting dangerously close to the gorge to issue that threat, you have +1 to the roll._ 

Each Experience can be called into play only once per Session, and only when the circumstances are especially fitting, interesting, or dramatic. 

At the end of a Shot, the Director can also ask you to erase or modify one of your Experiences when it is no longer fitting or interesting.
