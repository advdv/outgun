- ♦ **Cumulative Successes:** The Director determines that a single Success would be enough to get something right, but any additional Success can lead to even better results. The Adventurers can keep making Action Rolls until one of them fails, or until the Director proclaims that they have already reached the best possible outcome or run out of time. 

This method is useful for when the Adventurers are trying to build or repair something. 

**Alaska:** _I’m certain that I can get this old seaplane back in flying shape. I start tinkering with the engine. I roll Focus+Fix and get a Critical Success._ 

**Director:** _With a good dose of elbow grease, you get the engine going. The seaplane can fly, but the tear in the left wing could become a Hindrance if you meet any turbulence._ 

   - **Alaska:** _We still have some time, I want to patch that wing up. I think I saw some metal sheets around here._ 

- ♦ **Consecutive Successes:** The Director determines that the Adventurers must pass 2 consecutive rolls in order to succeed. Similarly, if the Adventurers fail 2 consecutive rolls, their efforts were in vain. 

This method is designed to manage small chase scenes, like the ones when the Adventurers need to evade a group of museum guards, or when they must manage to drive up to a jeep and jump on board. 

**Jason:** _Well, the cat’s out of the bag. I grab the sack and start running. I roll Brawn+Stunt and score a pretty failure._ 

**Director:** _You crash into a market stall and roll on the pavement. When you get back to your feet, you see the Rival’s goons are close. Another failure and they will grab you for sure!_
