Help & Hindrance<br><!-- End of picture text -->

When you're sinking in quicksand and can’t seem to find anything within reach, you can always rely on your trusty whip. However, even your most elegant clothes can become a hindrance when you need to leg it. 

Over the course of your adventure, you will learn quickly that everything, even your friends, can be a **Help** or a **Hindrance** , heavily influencing the result of your actions. 

# Help 

Some pieces of gear or Secondary Characters can grant you **Help** in specific circumstances. Your fellow Adventurers can also forgo rolling the dice to spend their turn trying to Help you face a difficult challenge. When you can rely on the Help of something or someone, the Director can grant you one of the following benefits, depending on the scope of the help: 

- ♦ **Gain +1** to an Action or Reaction Roll. **Director:** _Forcing this lock to open won’t be easy. Fortunately, you always carry your favorite set of lockpicks. You gain +1 to your Dexterity roll._ 

- ♦ **Gain a Success** automatically, without rolling the dice. **Director:** _Jason boosts you up and Alaska grabs your hand, hoisting you over the wall. Thanks to the Help of your friends, getting to the other side of the wall is easy as cake. You don’t even need to roll for it._ 

- ♦ **Gain the Requisite** to make a roll. **Director:** _Climbing this smooth wall of ice with your bare hands would be nearly impossible. Fortunately, you have a grappling hook, an essential Help that allows you to make this roll._ 

The Director should grant a **+1** to the roll, when an Adventurer can rely on significant but not decisive Help. When the Help is great enough to nullify the risks and possibilities of failure, then it’s best to grant an **automatic success** . 

Finally, when Help is instrumental to the action an Adventurer is attempting, the Director can **allow the Adventurer to make a roll** but grant no further bonus. 

Whenever in doubt, if an Adventurer can rely on some Help, the Director should always grant a +1 to the roll and let the dice decide.
