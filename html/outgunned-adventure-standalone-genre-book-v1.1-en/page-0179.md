# **FORCE OF NATURE [3 FP]** 

_The Enemy is huge and powerful, or they can rely on supernatural strength._ Adventurers suffer -1 to all Brawn rolls made against the Enemy, and on all Opposed Rolls where brute strength plays a role. 

# **IMMORTAL [3 FP]** 

_The Enemy is undead, a revenant, or another creature who doesn’t fear death._ The Enemy is immune to bullets, explosions, and fire damage, additionally, they do not need to breathe. 

# **SURGE [2 FP]** 

_You barely have time to realize what is happening, and the Enemy is already on you._ The Enemy can use a Special Action immediately when combat begins. 

# **SWARM [1 FP]** 

_The Enemy can dissolve into a swarm of insects, a cloud of bats, or into a swirling vortex of sand or fog._ 

The Enemy can move through cage bars and narrow spaces. 

Once per Turn, when the Enemy loses Grit, the swarm disperses. The next Adventurer to attack suffers -1 to their roll to strike. 

# **TAIL [2 FP]** 

_The Enemy can rely on a long tail, or a similar extra limb._ 

Add +1 Basic to the Enemy Attack (See **Piling up Problems** in **Section 3: DANGERS & ENEMIES** ). 

An Adventurer who wields a bladed weapon can cut off the Enemy’s tail by succeeding in an Action Roll with Difficulty equal to the Enemy’s Defense. 

# **WALKING CORPSE [1 FP]** 

_No longer alive, but not dead yet, this Enemy has risen from their tomb._ Adventurers suffer -1 when rolling to hit the Enemy with firearms or ranged weapons. Once defeated, the Enemy must be dismembered before 3 Turns have passed, or they will rise again with half of their Grit.
