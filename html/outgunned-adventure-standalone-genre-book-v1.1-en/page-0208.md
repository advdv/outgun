# TREASURE GUARDiANS 

# You can’t keep the Treasure 

Just like in adventure movies, at the end of an Outgunned Adventure campaign, the Adventurers never go home with the Treasure. First of all, stealing the Treasure is wrong, and on top of that, it can be extremely dangerous. 

The best finale for your breathtaking adventure should have all Adventurers learn what is truly important to them. 

Whether that is love, friendship, respect for life or themselves, or a genuine desire to do the right thing, in the end, true Adventurers will always discover something that is worth much more than a stuffy Treasure. 

Each Treasure is guarded by the walls of a temple or by a wild force of nature, and they are all surrounded by deadly traps. But some of them are also watched over by an ancient order of guardians, sworn to keep curious explorers and tomb robbers at bay. 

Often, these guardians **act in the shadows** , using an intricate web of spies to keep an eye on the area where the Treasure is located. These faceless protectors are always the first to know when someone sets off in search of the Treasure, but they only join the fray when things get really dangerous. 

Some guardian orders are still beholden to **ancient traditions** , wielding ceremonial weapons from long-forgotten eras. These warriors with extraordinary skills are always ready to intervene as soon as a stranger enters their territory, and they are often the direct descendants of the people who hid the Treasure in the first place. 

Guardians tend to be less-than-friendly with the Adventurers, but they can also become invaluable allies for those who have the best intentions and prove they are worthy of trust. 

Be very careful, however, as a Rival who can rely on **Control of the Territory** as one of their Strong Spots could have deceived the guardians to get them on their side!
