- ♦ **Best of 3/5:** The Director determines that, in order to succeed in a task, the Adventurers must score a success in 2 out of 3 rolls (or 3 out of 5). These rolls could even have different difficulties. If the Adventurers fail 2 out of 3 rolls (or 3 out of 5), it means their efforts failed. 

This method is perfect for sport or point-based competitions, or when the Adventurers have to withstand extreme conditions, or hold their breath for a prolonged period of time. 

**Raymond:** _I had underestimated my opponent, but I won’t make the same mistake twice. I roll Focus+ Know and get a Critical Success. I knew it, nobody beats me at chess._ 

**Director:** _You failed your first roll, and now got a beautiful success. The match continues, no holds barred, and it’s not yet clear who will get to mate first. Get ready, the next roll will determine the winner._ 

- ♦ **Failure Threshold:** The Director secretly determines that the Adventurers can only fail a set number of times, usually 1, 2, or 3 times, before the task is failed. If the Adventurers are facing the task as a group, it usually only takes a failure by one of them for things to turn out badly. 

This method is useful for when Adventurers are trying to sneak into a place, or when they must cross an unstable boardwalk. 

**Raymond:** _I take off my shoes and tip-toe around the sleeping soldiers, trying to make no noise at all. I roll Crime+Stealth and get a Critical Success._ 

**Alaska:** _I take a deep breath and follow Raymond carefully watching where I step. I roll Crime+Stealth and score no success at all._ 

**Director:** _Despite her careful efforts, the holster of Alaska’s gun bumps into the nose of a soldier, who mumbles in his sleep and turns to the other side. It seems like you're safe, for now, but who knows how many mistakes you can afford._
