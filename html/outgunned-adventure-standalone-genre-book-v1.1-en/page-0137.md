## **MELEE WEAPON** 

If you are not trained with them, melee weapons grant you no special advantage, but they are essential if you have the Favored Weapon Feat. 

## **BULKY** 

The weapon or piece of gear is especially big or heavy, it poses a Hindrance when you need to move quickly, climb, or swim. 

## **FIXED** 

The weapon is fixed on the ground or on a ride, it cannot be moved or carried. Fixed weapons have no mags and can keep shooting until they are destroyed. 

# Tactical weapons and rides 

In the Outgunned corebook, you can find advanced rules to manage weapon ranges and complex rides, which are more typical of the Action genre. 

## **SHARP** 

The weapon grants Help to cut ropes, carve wooden objects, and to cut through the vegetation. 

## **BOOM!** 

The weapon has powerful explosive blasts, it grants Help to crash through obstacles, gates, rides, or walls. 

Enemies who sustain a hit from a weapon with this Feat always lose 1 additional Grit, but Adventurers nearby will also run the risk of becoming Hurt. 

In Outgunned Adventure we keep things simple, but if you’d rather have a more tactical experience with some added rules, you can always try out the advanced options, or maybe even mix and match options for a personalized experience and a more varied toolkit!
