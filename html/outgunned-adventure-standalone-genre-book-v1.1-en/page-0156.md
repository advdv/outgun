# _Mechanical Statue_ 

_Standing before the entrance there are two statues holding swords, you feel as if they are watching you. When you try to enter, the statues spring into action, attacking you._ 

`All Adventurers within range of the mechanical statues must make a Critical Reaction Roll` @ `in Brawn+Fight to dodge or parry the attack.` 

```
In case of failure, the Adventurers get pushed to the ground or
grazed by the swords and lose Grit. Additionally, those who fail
to score at least a Basic Success also drop their weapon.
```
