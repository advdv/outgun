The Scoundrel<br><!-- End of picture text -->

_A lovable crook, an urchin who came up on the street, or a gentleman thief._ 

Most Adventurers survive thanks to their clear reasoning, steady nerves, muscles of steel, and a generous dose of luck. Scoundrels, on the other hand, make their way through cluttered cities, unexplored jungles, and buried pyramids thanks to nothing but their brazenness. 

These quick-fingered Adventurers with charming smiles can brave any challenge with levity, and while they might not have the heart of a lion, they are always in control of the situation. 

**Background:** 

Criminal, Former Soldier, Salesperson 

**Catchphrase: Flaw:** Do you trust me? I never tell the whole truth I’ve never seen this man before I have a secret It’s fine, they will never find us I never go first
