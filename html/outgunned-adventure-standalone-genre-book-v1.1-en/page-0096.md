# Multiple Difficulty Rolls 



Rolls with multiple difficulty allow you to manage complex situations in a single roll of the dice, to raise the stakes of an action, and to create brand new, thrilling scenes. 

**Director:** _You’re hanging by a branch, feet dangling into the void. Looking below you, you notice that you’re much higher than you realized._ 

**Jason:** _Hoping that the branch will hold my weight and trying to be very, very careful, I start pulling myself up._ 

**Director:** _Hoisting yourself onto an unstable branch as it swings on the precipice is no easy feat, and to make matters worse, a curious monkey is approaching. The primate walks over your hands like it was nothing and, after inspecting your hair, starts digging through your backpack._ 

Rolls with multiple difficulty also allow you to widen the range of difficulties in the game even further. For example, if a Critical roll feels like too little of a challenge but an Extreme would be overkill, you can ask for a Critical + Basic!
