# _Why Does Everything Need to Collapse?!_ 

```
Either due to their own greed or to a twist of Fate, the Adven-
turers ultimately end up falling toward the unfathomable depths
with the ship. This always happens!
```

```
Allow the Adventurers to devise their own way out. They could
swing from a rope hanging by the hull of the ship, or climb on the
main mast to reach a frozen wall, or, why not, even improvise a
parachute using the ship’s sails and the warm air that blows from
the deep. The crazier their plan, the more epic their escape!
```
