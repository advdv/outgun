# Enemy Grit 

The most important stat for an Enemy is their Grit, which is represented by a string of 3 to 12 boxes. The Director is free to choose how much Grit each Enemy has. A higher number of Grit boxes will cause the fight to last longer. 

As a general rule, Enemies with Basic or Critical Defense should have between 3 and 6 Grit boxes. The Director can add boxes, up to a total of 12, when the Enemy is especially strong, or has great numbers. 

Enemies with Extreme Defense usually have 3 Grit, and should never have more than 6 Grit boxes. 

When the Adventurers land a hit on the Enemy, the Director fills in a Grit box, and **when all boxes are full, the Enemy is defeated** or forced to flee. 

Remember that an Enemy could represent a single tough opponent, or a large number of ugly mugs. You should adapt the way you narrate lost Grit depending on the description of the Enemy. 

If the Enemy is indeed an individual, lost Grit could represent bruises, wounds, or hard blows. But if the Enemy is a group of soldiers, each instance of lost Grit could represent one soldier getting knocked out. 

# Enemy Defense 

The Defense score of an Enemy represents the difficulty of Action Rolls that Adventurers need to land blows. Whenever you score a success equal to the Defense, the Enemy loses 1 Grit. If you score a higher success, the Enemy loses 3 Grit. 

For example, if you’re attacking an Enemy with Critical Defense and score a Critical Success, the Enemy loses 1 Grit. If you score an Extreme Success, the Enemy loses 3 Grit. If you score a Basic Success, you cannot land a blow on the Enemy. 

The Director can set the Enemy Defense as: 

- ♦ **Basic:** The Enemy is very easy to hit or wound. This is ideal for low-stakes combat scenes, or situations where weaker Adventurers are fighting alone. 

- ♦ **Critical:** The Enemy is a tough nut, and wounding them is no easy feat. Most Enemies worthy of the name have Critical Defense. 

- ♦ **Extreme:** The Enemy’s Defense is almost perfect. It will be extremely difficult for Adventurers to land blows, let alone defeat them.
