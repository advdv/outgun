# _Classic Deadly Traps_ 

## _Flooded Room_ 

_It’s as if someone opened up a thousand faucets, flooding the room with freezing cold water. Before you can understand what is going on, the water has already reached your ankles._ 

The room is quickly flooding with water, this sets off an **`Extended Roll`** `with a Timed Success Threshold (4 Turns). To save themselves, the Adventurers must score a total of 5 Critical Successes with their Action Rolls in order to deactivate the mechanism or open a way out.` 

```
Every turn, the Director asks the Adventurers to make a Critical
Reaction Roll , such as:
```

```
You are now submerged in water and must hold your breath.
Roll Brawn+Endure.
```

`The water hinders your movements.` **`Roll Brawn+Survival.`** You must find shelter or high ground. **`Roll Nerves+Detect.`** 

Starting from the third Turn, the room is completely flooded. The `Adventurers must hold their breath and deal with a` **`Hindrance`** `.` 

```
In case of failure, the Adventurers lose Grit and can suffer the
Frozen, Stunned, or Hurt Conditions.
```

`If the Adventurers fail the Extended Roll, at the beginning of` the fifth Turn they end up with no oxygen left in their lungs and `sealed in the room, until an ally, an enemy, or fortune intervenes to take them back to dry land.` 

```
The Adventurers suffer the Broken Condition or get captured and
the Heat rises by 1.
```
