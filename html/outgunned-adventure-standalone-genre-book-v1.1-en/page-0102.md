# Uncommon Conditions 



Beyond the core Conditions already included on the Adventurer Sheet, your journeys might lead you to encounter other, more uncommon Conditions. 

Here you find a list of 5 uncommon Conditions, including the exceptional positive Condition “Relaxed”, but your group is always free to come up with new and different Conditions in order to customize your gaming experience. 

## **RELAXED** 

## **When you should suffer the next Condition, you lose the Relaxed Condition instead.** 

_With many adversities now behind you, you feel refreshed in body and spirit._ **You can get this Condition** when a friend uses one of their actions while at Camp to **Comfort** the group. When you receive an especially pleasant or luxurious treatment, the Director can also grant you this unusual positive Condition. **You must remove this Condition** the next time you stand to suffer any other Condition, or at the beginning of your next Camp scene. The Director can also require you to remove this Condition when enough time has elapsed between two Scenes or Shots, e.g. after a long journey at sea. 

## **POISONED** 

**At the beginning of each Turn, flip a coin. Heads: You lose 1 Grit.** 

**If you’ve Lost all Grit and should lose additional Grit because of this Condition, you become Broken instead of taking a spin on the Death Roulette as usual.** _You’re sweating cold, can hardly breathe, or your head is spinning._ 

**You can get this Condition** when you inhale, ingest, or touch a poisonous substance, or when you are wounded by a poisoned weapon. 

**You can remove this Condition** by finding the right antidote. While at Camp, either you or another Adventurer can use one of your actions to create the right antidote with a Critical Action Roll in Focus+Heal, so long as you have the necessary tools. For serious cases, the Director could determine that crafting the antidote will require an extremely rare ingredient.
