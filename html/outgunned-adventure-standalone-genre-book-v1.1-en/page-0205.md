# Sequel, Prequel, Spin-Off 

adventure<sup>is designed for short to medium Cinematic Campaigns that strive</sup> to keep tensions always high. But once your adventure is over, there is nothing stopping you from setting off right away in search of another Treasure. 

The most obvious option is to keep the story going by putting up a **Sequel** , which is a Cinematic Campaign taking place after the one you have just played. But why not try a **Prequel** so you can learn something new about the Adventurers’ backgrounds? 

You can also play a **Spin-Off** , a story that focuses on the life of only one of the Adventurers, or perhaps of your favorite supporting character. 

Are you ready for your own thrilling **Cinematic Saga** full of adventure? 

# Travel Journal 

The **Travel Journal** is a special group Sheet that allows you to keep a log of everything that happens during your adventure, tracking the Heat and noting the clues you found about the Rival or the Treasure. 

There’s also a Shot section, where you can write down short summaries of your Shots so as to not forget anything.
