# WEAPON AND GEAR FEATS 

## **ACCURATE** 

This weapon is especially precise. You can spend one Turn to take aim at a target. On your next Turn, you gain +1 to your roll to hit that target. 

# Counting Shots 

I know I said that we do not count bullets in Outgunned Adventure, but every rule needs an exception. 

When you want to underline how dramatic a scene is or you want to give more gravitas to a milestone when the Adventurers need to survive on very limited resources, it could be best to change approach. 

First of all, warn the Adventurers of how many projectiles they have left, then watch their strategy change as things get real. 

## **SINGLE SHOT** 

The gun doesn’t use a Mag but single munitions. Ignore Mags and keep track of munitions in your equipment. 

## **SILENT** 

The weapon is very quiet. When shooting, you don’t alert the enemy and don’t reveal your position. 

## **RAPID FIRE** 

When laying down Covering Fire you also gain +1 to your next Reaction Roll. 

## **SLOW RELOAD** 

The weapon requires a longer time to be reloaded. You need a Full Action instead of a Quick Action in order to reload the gun. 

## **UNCOMMON** 

The weapon or gear is hard to come by. The Director is tasked with determining if and how you can find or craft the item you are looking for and what the price will be, based on the circumstances. 

## **IMPACT** 

The weapon grants Help to crash through doors and objects and to hurl Enemies away.
