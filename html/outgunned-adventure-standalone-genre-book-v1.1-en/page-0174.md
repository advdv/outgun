# **Truth or Superstition?** 

Before you set off on your next adventure, ask yourself this question: is the supernatural real? 

- ♦ **Yes, but it doesn’t appear to be:** The Adventurers have never made contact with anything supernatural before, and they will need tangible proof before accepting the kind of trouble they are now in. If you choose this option, you need to ration mysterious events and plausible explanations carefully, always leaving room for doubt. Only in the very end will the supernatural reveal itself in its full, unfathomable force. 

   - **_Cinematic Examples:_** _Raiders of the Lost Ark, the Last Crusade_ 

- ♦ **No, but it appears to be:** There are many events with no apparent explanation, and at one point, the Adventurers come to believe there must be an otherworldly force at play. The truth is that there is actually a logical (sort of) and scientific explanation to everything, but they will only discover this at the very end. The undead army they had to fight? Gravely ill soldiers. The legendary guardians? Obtained their strength thanks to a medicinal plant. **_Cinematic Examples:_** _Uncharted (videogame), Tomb Raider_ 

- ♦ **Yes, and it appears to be:** The supernatural elements are clear from the very beginning, leaving no room for doubt. The Adventurers know what they are dealing with, and they are too busy fighting mythical beasts and Egyptian plagues to ask questions. 

   - **_Cinematic Examples:_** _The Mummy (1999), Jumanji_ 

- ♦ **No, and it doesn’t appear to be:** Those are only legends and superstitions. I know it, you know it, the Adventurers know it, and nobody ever doubts this. No harm in a little fiction, but let’s keep it within reason. **_Cinematic Examples:_** _National Treasure, The Lost City_ 

- ♦ **Nobody knows:** You have yet to decide, and that’s just fine. Add a mysterious element or a suspicious clue here and there, and watch for the Adventurers’ reaction. Was that a yeti or just a huge guy in furs? Who knows! You can wait until the very end to decide if there’s any truth to the legends.
