# When to Advance 

Adventurers can advance: 

- ♦ **After a Point of No Return:** At the end of a Shot that marks a Point of No Return, the Adventurers are even more determined than ever, and they have digested their recent experiences. 

♦ **Before the Moment of Truth:** Before the beginning of the final Shot and the Moment of Truth, the Adventurers feel the Treasure is close. It might feel strange to have Adventurers advance before their final challenge, but when the crucial moment comes, they are always ready for it. Just like in the movies! 

# How much to Advance 

Each Adventurer can advance 3 times, gaining a total of 6 Skill Points and 3 additional Feats. 

When an Adventurer who has already advanced 3 times is about to face a Point of No Return or Moment of Truth, the Player can choose to reassign 2 Skill Points of their choice and/or swap out one of their Feats for another one of their choosing. If they choose to do neither, they gain 1 additional Luck. 

# Experiences 

If the Advancement is a mechanical representation of the Adventurer’s growth and of their skills improving, Experiences mark their personal growth and the accomplishments of their adventures. 

The heart of an Adventurer’s evolution clearly doesn’t lie in a handful of additional dice. It’s all in the Experiences they gather along the way. 

# SLOW ADVANCEMENT 

Outgunned Adventure 

is designed for short to medium length Campaigns that usually span no more than 8 Sessions, as such, the  advancement curve is pretty fast and steep. 

If you wish to expand your adventure, you can also slow down the Adventurers’ 

Advancement, making them work harder for their growth. 

For their first advancement, the Adventurers will only gain 1 Feat of their choice, for the second, they will gain 2 Skill Points, and so on. This will allow for up to 6 advancements over the course of a single Campaign!
