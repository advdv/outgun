The road of an Adventurer is paved with more than Feats and Skill Points to add to your Sheet. Along the journey, you will also collect Experiences. 

An **Experience** is a short sentence that represents a crucial event of your life that changed you deeply. 

When you face a challenge that leaves a mark, when you survive a desperate encounter, when you take part in a memorable exchange, or when you make an upsetting discovery, the Director can ask you to note down the Experience on your Adventurer Sheet. 

There are 4 types of Experiences: 

**Achievement:** You have learned an important lesson, or accomplished something great. 

_“I survived the blizzard.”_ 

**Scar:** You’ve suffered physically, endured mental trauma, or were hugely disappointed. 

_“I couldn’t save you.”_ 

**Bond:** You made a bond with someone. You earned a friend, lover, or rival. _“I can trust Alaska.”_ 

**Reputation:** You’ve earned a good or bad reputation. People might have heard about you. 

_“I made a mockery of the Nazis.”_
