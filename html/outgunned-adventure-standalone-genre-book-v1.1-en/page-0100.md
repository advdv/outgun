# Core Conditions 



## **TIRED** 

**You suffer no penalty** , but are one step closer to looking Broken. _You can’t catch your breath and your back aches, or you're in shambles and walk head down._ 

**You can get this Condition** when you exert yourself too much, when you go too long without eating or drinking, or when you stay up all night without getting any rest. When you fill in your Bad Box in a not-so-bad situation, the Director can also choose to give you the Tired Condition. 

**You can remove this Condition** by eating a hot meal and getting some good sleep. 

## **HURT** 

## **-1 to all rolls in Brawn.** 

_You have a fracture, a deep cut, or a projectile lodged somewhere._ 

**You can get this Condition** when you really take a beating, when you fall from a great height, or when you’re blown up. 

**You can remove this Condition** by seeking adequate medical care. Alternatively, while at Camp, a friend can use one of their actions to treat you with a Critical Action Roll in Focus+Heal. You can try to treat yourself, but you always roll with -1. 

## **NERVOUS** 

## **-1 to all rolls in Nerves.** 

_You’re stressed out, your hands are shaking, or you’re sweating bullets._ 

**You can get this Condition** when you survive danger by an inch, or when you’re under a great deal of stress. 

**You can remove this Condition** at Camp by using one of your actions to relax, enjoy a hobby, or indulge a bit. 

## **LIKE A FOOL** 

## **-1 to all rolls in Smooth.** 

_You're deeply embarrassed, or feel inadequate or ridiculous._ 

**You can get this Condition** when someone embarrasses you, when you cover yourself in ridicule, or lose face in front of others. 

**You can remove this Condition** by proving your worth, getting some recognition, or earning the respect of someone. Alternatively, while at Camp, a friend can use one of their actions to cheer you up and help you find your self-esteem with a Critical Action Roll in Smooth+Heal.
