The Professor<br><!-- End of picture text -->

_A disheveled researcher who dedicated their entire life to one discovery, a professor with elbow patches, or a young bookworm chasing their dreams._ 

Not all Adventurers are trained in the field, Professors, for example, spend their best years with their nose buried in books to the point that they practically become walking encyclopedias. Sooner or later, however, adventure will knock on their door and force them to join the fray to solve a great mystery, or to crown their life-long research. 

**Background:** 

University Professor, Librarian, Researcher 

**Catchphrase: Flaw:** I’ve read it in a book I always have a theory to test We never stop learning I don’t know when to shut up The pen is mightier than the sword Without my glasses, I am nearly blind
