# List of Feats 

## **ARCHEOLOGY** 

_You know history and the myths that surround it like the back of your hand._ Gain a Free Re-roll for all rolls regarding archeology, dead languages, or the history, culture, and legends of ancient civilizations. 

## **ARTIST** 

_Popular or misunderstood, you’re still an artist. The world is your stage._ Choose an art form (e.g.: dancing, singing, painting, etc.) Gain a Free Re-roll for all rolls regarding your chosen art form. 

## **BIG AND STRONG** 

_You’re unbelievably tall, large, and bulky._ Your considerable size grants you Help whenever you try to lift or break objects, but it poses a Hindrance when you try to hide in small spaces or balance your mass in unsteady situations. 

## **BODYGUARD** 

_You step in at the last second to help your friend, disregarding your own safety and health._ 

After another Adventurer fails a roll, you suffer the consequences of their failure (for example, if your friend should lose Grit or suffer a Condition, you lose Grit or suffer the Condition in their stead). 

## **CHIN UP** 

_Even in the darkest times, your friends can always rely on you._ **[Full Turn]** Remove the Scared or Like a Fool Condition, or another emotional Condition from an ally. Alternatively, while at Camp, you can take the Cheer Up action without needing to roll the dice. 

## **DISGUISE** 

_In the bag, you see an officer’s uniform. And it’s in your size._ 

**[Full Turn]** Find, steal, or craft the perfect disguise for yourself and/or one other Adventurer without rolling the dice.
