

# **The Fortune Seeker** 

_An adventurer searching for their destiny, for justice, or for the truth._ 

Fortune Seekers are the be-all and end-all of an Adventurer. They have a strong personal tie with the story or the Treasure, and often some unfinished business with the Rival too. 

Gifted, trained, and blessed by luck, Fortune Seekers have everything it takes to find the Treasure before it falls into the wrong hands. 

**Catchphrase: Flaw:** Could we talk about it later? I never know when to stop I’m sorry, I have to do this I always put the Treasure first I am the master of my own destiny I have an eyepatch 

## Caracteristics of a Fortune Seeker 

RESOLUTE. DOES THE RIGHT THING. LEGACY.
