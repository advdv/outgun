# **DISTRACTED** 

# **-1 to all rolls in Focus.** 

_You're lost in your thoughts, your mind is foggy, you’re a little out of it and can’t seem to keep your focus._ 

**You can get this Condition** when you ruminate about a problem for too long without finding a solution, when you let yourself be charmed or confused, or when you spend a night hitting the books or at the workbench and feel dizzy as a result. **You can remove this Condition** by choosing to spend 2 Luck on any Focus roll. Alternatively, while at Camp, you can spend one of your actions to do absolutely nothing and clear your mind. 

# **SCARED** 

# **-1 to all rolls in Crime.** 

_Your eyes are wide open, your palms are sweaty, or you’re uncharacteristically clumsy._ 

**You can get this Condition** when you suffer a great shock or fright, or when you narrowly avoid a painful death by sheer luck. 

**You can remove this Condition** by facing the subject of your fear again and being victorious. Alternatively, while at Camp, you can spend one of your actions to confide in one of your friends and get the weight off your chest... So long as another Adventurer is also available to spend one action to listen to you and help you through it. 

# **BROKEN** 

# **-1 to all rolls.** 

_You’re broken in mind, body, or spirit, or are in downright terrible shape._ **You get this Condition** when you should suffer a fourth Condition. 

When you already have 3 Conditions and stand to get a fourth, you look Broken instead. You cannot suffer other Conditions until you remove one. 

If you already look Tired and Hurt and suffer another great physical blow, the Director can also choose to give you the Broken Condition. 

**You can remove this Condition** by spending a couple of days in a hospital or another similar institution. Alternatively, while at Camp, a friend can use one of their actions to treat your wounds with an Extreme Action Roll in Focus+Heal.
