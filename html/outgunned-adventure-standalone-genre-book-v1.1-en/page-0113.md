

# Reaction Turn during combat 

During a Reaction Turn, the Director describes the action of the Enemy and asks all Adventurer involved in combat to make a **Reaction Roll** . 

This means that all Reaction Rolls made during combat are Dangerous Rolls. Seems obvious. 

In combat the difficulty of Reaction Rolls is equal to the **Enemy Attack** . 

As such, if you are fighting against an Enemy with Basic Attack, you must make a Basic Reaction Roll or risk losing 1 Grit. If the Enemy has Critical Attack, you must make a Critical Reaction Roll and run the risk of losing 3 Grit. 

As usual, you can spend any smaller success rolled during combat to do **Damage Control** . 

If you have any doubt on the subject of Rolls or losing Grit, run back to the chapter on **Dangerous Rolls** in **Section 3: DANGERS & ENEMIES** . 

If you don’t know what roll to ask the Adventurers to make, here is a handy list of examples. Feel free to come up with your own Reaction Rolls, choose from this list, or even roll at the beginning of the turn to randomize the Turn. 

**1.** The enemies attack you with fists and bats. **Roll Brawn+Fight** 

**2.** The enemies shoot at you. **Roll Brawn+Stunt** 

**3.** The enemies try to grapple or choke you. **Roll Brawn+Endure** 

**4.** The enemies try to push you the ground. **Roll Brawn+Force** 

**5.** The enemies throw tables and chairs at you. **Roll Nerves+Stealth** 

**6.** The enemies try to surround you. **Roll Nerves+Awareness** 

As a general rule, ALL Adventurers taking part in combat must make the same Reaction Roll. If an Adventurer is outside of the Enemy’s range, the Director can decide that they automatically pass the Reaction Roll. At least until someone reaches them, or directs fire onto them.
