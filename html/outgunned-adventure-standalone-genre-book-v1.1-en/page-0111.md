# Winning Combat 

When the Director fills in the last Grit box for an Enemy, it means that the Adventurers have won the combat. 

The Enemy goes K.O. or is forced to flee. Serves them right! 

# Losing Combat 

If all Adventurers actively participating in a combat scene lose all their Grit, it means that they were defeated. 

When this happens the Enemy surrounds and captures them, or they are pushed into a corner and forced to either negotiate or attempt a desperate escape.
