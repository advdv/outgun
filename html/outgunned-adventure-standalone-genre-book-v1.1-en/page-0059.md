## **MONEYBAGS** 

_You inherited a fortune, or started at rock bottom and built a fortune for yourself._ 

You start the game with 4 Cash. **[Full Turn]** You can spend 1 Luck to find a contact, passage to somewhere, an invite to an event, or another service thanks to your huge wealth or fame. 

## **PHYSICIAN** 

_You’re a nurse, medical doctor, or army medic. You’re certainly not squeamish about blood._ 

Gain a Free Re-roll for all rolls made to find a diagnosis, to operate on someone, or to remove physical Conditions such as the Hurt Condition. 

## **PILOT** 

_You feel truly at peace only when you’re above the clouds._ 

Gain a Free Re-roll when piloting, repairing, or evaluating flying rides. 

## **QUICK AND NIMBLE** 

_You are lithe, thin, or especially slight._ Your slender frame grants you Help whenever you try to hide, sneak, or balance, but it poses a Hindrance when you try to lift or break objects. 

## **QUICK FINGERS** 

_If you want something, you just need to get your hands on it._ 

Gain a Free Re-roll when using sleight of hand. **[Full Turn]** You can spend 1 Luck to immediately retrieve a small item you lost during this Session, or to ensure a small object goes unnoticed during a search or check. 

# Free Re-roll 

Some Feats that grant you a Free Re-roll leave some room for interpretation and require you to use some creativity. For such cases, you should always try to call the Feat in to play when it sounds useful, without getting too greedy. 

For example, the Maverick Feat grants you a Free Re-roll whenever you go against the wishes of the group. It’s okay to take advantage of this 

Feat when you have a plan that is brilliant and dangerous and the others don’t trust you, 

or when you make a controversial decision. But you can’t call it into play whenever you do something different than the others!
