# _The Chintamani Stone_ 

`The Chintamani is a legendary jewel, its name can be literally` translated from Sanskrit as “wish-fulfilling stone”. According to `Hinduist traditions, the Chintamani is a bright pearl set in the crown of the Naga King, leader of a mythical race of snake-people. According to Tibetan Buddhists, the stone is a powerful symbol of compassion, which is why it is often depicted in the hand of the Buddha.` 

`While some people believe the Chintamani Stone to be nothing but an unbelievably precious pearl, others think it is a miraculous` artifact that can fulfill any wish, almost everyone still agrees `that it did actually exist at some point, and it is hidden at the` heart of a city nobody can find, protected by an ancient race of `guardians. Most people maintain that this city is Shambala, also known as Shangri-La, a paradise-like oasis hidden among the most unreachable peaks of the Himalayas.`
