The Captain<br><!-- End of picture text -->

_A veteran ready to dive back into the action, a skilled expedition leader, a fearless aviator, or a sailor with years of experience._ 

Whether they are piloting a ramshackle seaplane, steering an old steamboat through the jungle, or leading their companions on a desperate expedition, Captains are always ready for a generous helping of adventure. 

Some Captains are the leaders of their group, others are free spirits who never set foot on land. But one thing is clear: when the situation calls for it, they will be ready to take the wheel, yoke, rudder, or any alternative. 

**Background:** 

Aviator, Ship Captain, Veteran
