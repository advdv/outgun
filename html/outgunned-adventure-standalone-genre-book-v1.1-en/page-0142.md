# _The Serpent Dagger_ 

`The Serpent Dagger is technically a phurba,` a finely decorated ancient ceremonial dag `- ger typical of Nepal and Tibet. The blade is carved with clues in Sanskrit, and nestled in the pommel there is a green gem that looks like an emerald. When the dagger is placed on the right altar, the light of dawn can pass through the gem and turn into a beacon that shows the way to Shangri-La.`
