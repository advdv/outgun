# Hindrance 

Bulky or inadequate gear, supporting characters who need your help or work against you, and unfavorable circumstances can all become **Hindrances** to your adventure. 

When someone or something poses a Hindrance, the Director can levy one of the following drawbacks, depending on the circumstances: 

- ♦ **Suffer -1** to an Action or Reaction Roll. **Director:** _The old man is wounded and limping, but you can't just leave him. It’s not his fault if he’s being a Hindrance, but he still imposes -1 to your roll to get away._ 

- ♦ **Get a Failure** automatically, without rolling the dice. **Director:** _Your companions push to squeeze into the tunnel, calling you to follow them. Unfortunately, you have the Big and Strong Feat, and there is no way that you will fit in there. I’m sorry, this is an automatic failure._ 

- ♦ **You require a Help** in order to even attempt the roll. **Director:** _When you reach the bottom of the rock wall, you realize that your backpack is way too full, it’s clearly a Hindrance. There’s no way you can make this climb bare-handed. Either someone lowers a rope for you, or you’ll have to ditch the dead weight._ 

The Director should impose -1 to the roll when the Hindrance is minor, but still impossible to ignore. On the other hand, when the Hindrance is serious enough to preclude your success, it’s fair for them to proclaim an **automatic failure** . 

Finally, when the Hindrance makes an action only temporarily impossible, the Director can state that the **Adventurer needs some outside Help** or must get rid of the cause of the hindrance before they can make the roll. 

As above, in case of doubt, the Director can always impose -1 to the roll without worrying too much about it.
