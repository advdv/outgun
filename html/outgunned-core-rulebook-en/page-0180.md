# Grit of a Supporting Character 

Each Supporting Character has 3 Grit points. 

# Spotlight on a Supporting Character 

In desperate or extreme situations, the Players or Director can choose to sacrifice a Supporting Character to gain the same benefits normally granted by a Spotlight. 

When this happens, the Supporting Character takes 

center stage and does something heroic or flat-out crazy in order to help the Heroes on their mission, before bowing out in epic or dramatic fashion. 

Once they’ve had their moment in the limelight, the Supporting Character is Left for Dead. 

But we will remember them always! 

Like Heroes, Supporting Characters who lost all Grit can’t sustain any more damage. If they lose any more Grit, they will be **Left for Dead** , and only a Spotlight can save them. 

A Supporting Character may lose 1 Grit point: 

- ♦ **After a failure** made by them or by the Heroes. 

- ♦ **Because of an Enemy’s attack** during a Reaction Turn. Supporting Characters don’t take part in every Reaction Turn, but can be forced to roll dice when attacked, or when the enemy is targeting them specifically. 

- ♦ **In dramatic circumstances** , for example, when they run too big of a risk, end up in the wrong place at the wrong time, or end up facing the wrong enemy. 

Keep in mind that Supporting Characters always lose 1 Grit at a time, regardless of how strong of a blow they take or how big their failure. 

Supporting Characters recover 1 Grit every time they Catch a Break or get a Time-Out. 

Additionally, a Hero can use one of their actions during a Time-Out to treat the wounds of a Supporting Character with a Critical Action Roll in Focus+Heal. If they succeed, the Character recovers 1 Grit.
