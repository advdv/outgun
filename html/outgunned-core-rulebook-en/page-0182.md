







# **The Heat** 

As your mission progresses, heat will rise. And I’m not talking about the midday sun. This is the kind of heat that has you sweating bullets when you must choose whether to cut the blue or red wire. 

Friendly advice: it’s never the red wire. 

Heat is a tracker with numbers from 1 to 12 and it represents how much danger the Heroes are in. 

This tool is designed to be used during Cinematic Campaigns, and it allows the Director to raise the stakes gradually as the Heroes’ mission proceeds. 

Whenever the Heroes fail to reach an objective, get delayed, or when the Villain wins, the Heat rises and the Heroes will have to deal with annoying setbacks or even be forced to add Lethal Bullets to their Death Roulette.
