# Enemy Templates 

For each Enemy Type you can choose one of 5 different Templates, each described by their own sheet. You’ll see that some Templates are stronger, while others can rely on more Grit. Others still are simply more powerful than other Enemies of their kind. For convenience, **Templates are listed in order of difficulty from 1 to 5** . 

This means Template 1 Goons are weaker than Template 5 Goons. And Template 1 Bad Guys are stronger than all Goons Templates. 

In the next pages you’ll find tables for all Templates of Goons, Bad Guys, and Bosses. When you need an Enemy, go to the table, choose one of the 3 Types, and then pick your favorite Template. 

# Playing Enemies 

As a Director, you are tasked with describing and playing the role of Enemies as you see fit. For example, you can choose to put into play some “Bad Guys - Template 1” and describe them to the Heroes as a group of burly batterers, or as a single highly-trained soldier, or even as three police officers on duty. 

Each Template includes some suggestions about what opponents it could represent, but feel free to use any Template to represent any type of henchman armed with bad intentions. And there’s nothing wrong with just describing them as “a group of bad guys” without really focusing on the number. Who keeps count anyway? 

# CANNON FODDER 

You’re a Director. You need Enemies. You don’t have time to read the list. Lucky for you, we have Cannon Fodder! 

Cannon Fodder is a special Type of Enemy with 9 Grit boxes, 2 Basic Attack, Basic Defense... That’s it. No Feats, no Special Actions. 

You can also try the spicy version, with Critical Attack and Critical Defense. A real challenge for any Hero. 

When you’re in a hurry, or whenever in doubt, toss in some Cannon Fodder!
