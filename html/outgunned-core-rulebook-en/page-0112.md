# Earning Cash 

# Two-bit Guns and Rides 

You're short on Cash but desperately need to find a gun or ride? You can always buy some two-bit gear. 

These items are old, broken, or unreliable, and they cost much less than brand-new products. Normally, a car costs 3$, but a two-bit car only costs 1$. It’s a great deal! A pistol costs 1$, but you can pay the same price and get three pieces of dubious origin. Such a bargain! 

Always remember that all two-bit guns have the Jam Feat, and all two-bit rides have a speed score of 0, and they could even start with 1 or 2 armor points missing. But I’m sure they won’t betray you at the worst possible moment. Probably. 

All Heroes start the game with 1$ in their pocket, and they can decide to spend it immediately to buy some additional gear. Some Roles and Tropes let you choose the Cash Flow Feat, which grants you additional Cash, no questions asked. 

During your mission, you can get additional Cash by picking up side-jobs, or by selling some of your possessions, or simply by borrowing it from your beloved enemies. 

Anyway, it’s hard for a Hero to get their hands on more than 1$ at a time, unless they are robbing a bank or some such thing. And when you re-sell an item, most buyers will only be willing to pay one third of the initial price. They also need to gain from the transaction!
