# Death Roulette 

The Death Roulette is a metaphorical cylinder that contains up to 6 Lethal Bullets. 

You begin the game with 1 Lethal Bullet, and gain another whenever you take a spin on the Death Roulette to save your bacon. The more bullets there are in your cylinder, the harder it will be for you to keep avoiding a dramatic departure from the scene.

# Cash & Gear 

During your mission, you can rely on a good number of guns, rides, and pieces of gear that will soon become your best friends. You begin the game with the equipment granted to you by your Role as well as 1 Cash, which you can spend during the mission to buy items or services. 

When creating your Hero, you can ask the Director to grant you one additional item of your choice, so long as it is truly useful or important for your Hero. I’m talking about cameras, family mementos, and tools of the trade... not about bazookas and attack helicopters. You hear me? 

Some Roles and Tropes also allow you to choose the **Cash Flow** Feat, which allows you to gain additional Cash at the beginning of every Session, including the first. 

# Loaded Weapons 

Some Roles include a nice set of firearms with your starting gear. For example, if you choose to play the Agent, you start the game with a pistol on your hip, and if you choose to enlist as a Commando, you can even choose your favorite weapon. 

When filling in your Hero Sheet, you immediately gain 2 Mags for each of your weapons. One is already in the gun, ready to open fire. The second can be noted down by filling in one of the three bullets beside the weapon in the Guns & Gear box on your sheet.
