# **bad guys** 

## **3 FEAT POINTS** 

Bad Guys know how to earn respect, they generally pose a challenge for most Heroes. Call Bad Guys with Templates 1 and 2 into play when you’re looking to wear down the Heroes, maybe adding a couple of Dangerous Rolls before or after combat. When you really want to keep Heroes on their toes, Templates 3, 4, and 5 are your best bet.
