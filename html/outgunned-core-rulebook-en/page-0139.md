# Special Actions 

Some Bad Guys and Bosses also have some **HOT BOXES** among their Grit boxes. 

When they fill in a Hot Box, the Director receives 1 Adrenaline, which they can spend whenever they want to make a **Special Action** . 

There are Special Actions that cost 1, 2, or 3 Adrenaline. The Director is free to choose whether to use Adrenaline immediately or stockpile it to make deadly Special Actions toward the end of combat. 

While Enemy Feats are used to tailor Enemies, Special Actions are intended as a way to spice up combat, creating new and surprising dynamics for the fight. Additionally, the Director is not required to choose before time if the Enemy will use a Special Action; they can simply collect Adrenaline and choose whether to spend it or not in the moment. 

You should use Special Actions when: 

- ♦ You want to extend a fight. 

- ♦ You want to cause unforeseen accidents. 

- ♦ You want to have some fun with the enemy strategy.

# Final Blow 

As a Director, there’s always some risk when trying to accumulate enough Adrenaline to use a devastating Special Action. Sometimes, one of those pesky Heroes scores a portentous success and gets rid of the Enemy before you can use the final move. When this happens, the Director just loses all Adrenaline and has to deal with it. 

Enemies who have a **HOT BOX** as their last Grit box, however, are an exception. In such cases, the Director can choose to spend their Adrenaline even after the Enemy is defeated. One last parting gift that will surely make an impression!
