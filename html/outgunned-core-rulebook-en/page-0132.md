# **goons** 

## **1 FEAT POINT** 

Goons are the weakest Type of Enemy, two-bit brawlers or low-ranking henchmen. Call Goons into play when you want Heroes to warm up a bit, or are trying to slow them down. 

These tattered Enemies are unlikely to win.
