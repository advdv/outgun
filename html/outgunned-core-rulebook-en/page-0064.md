> ROLL DiFFiCULTY ~~ee~~ The higher the difficulty, the higher success you’ll need to pass the roll. There are 4 levels of difficulty: 

- ♦ **BASIC:** A quick and easy action or reaction. Like jumping over an obstacle, stealing the keys off of a distracted agent, throwing something. 

- ♦ **CRITICAL:** A full action or reaction. Like bursting through a sturdy door, jumping out of a window, earning someone’s trust. 

- ♦ **EXTREME:** A truly demanding action or reaction. Like picking the lock of a secure vault, shooting a speeding target at night in the pouring rain, persuading a sworn enemy to cooperate. 

- ♦ **IMPOSSIBLE:** A desperate action or reaction. Like calmly exiting a car in the middle of a 180 while shooting at a tiny target, getting rid of a large group of enemies with nothing but a pencil, taking down a helicopter with a speeding car because you’re running low on ammo. Stuff from movie trailers.
