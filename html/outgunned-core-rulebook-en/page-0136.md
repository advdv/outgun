# **bosses** 

## **5 FEAT POINTS** 

Bosses are extremely strong, and there is a decent chance that Heroes will not prevail against one. As such, you should save Bosses for Turning Points and Showdowns. Templates 1, 2, and 3 play rough, but their Defense score is not too high. Templates 4 and 5 are truly excessive and, unless they can rely on several Spotlights, Heroes are in for a world of hurt.
