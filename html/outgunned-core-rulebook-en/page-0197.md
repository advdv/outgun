# Life-Changing Experiences 

Some Experiences just hit different, they draw a strong line between the person you were and the one you will be. In such cases, you can choose to change your Catchphrase or Flaw as a way to represent your Hero’s development. 

- ♦ Johnny is like a son to me 

- ♦ Bruce is just the best! 

- ♦ I’m envious of Mekia 

- ♦ Oleg gets on my nerves! 

# Reputation Examples

- ♦ I’ve robbed the mint 

- ♦ I’ve hacked into the CIA servers 

- ♦ I’ve sent my friends to jail 

- ♦ They call me “The Gentleman Thief” 

- ♦ I have never lost a fight
