







# **the Agent** 

_A police officer, a special agent from the FBI, a firefighter, a secret agent, or an undercover cop._ 

Agents are Heroes with a badge who work for a government agency of some kind. Some of them are by-the-book and uphold law and order, others are hotheads in search of justice. Many of them wear mirrored sunglasses and threadbare shirts. All of them have nerves of steel and are always ready to step in when duty calls. 

### **Job:** 

Police Officer, Government Agent, Double Agent 

**Catchphrase:** Not in my city Give it here, I’ll do it I’m not a hero, I’m just doing my job 

**Flaw:** I’m too arrogant and overconfident I always trust the wrong people I never accept help and don’t work well with others 

## characteristics of an agent 

BRAVE. PROTECTOR. HARD TO KILL.
