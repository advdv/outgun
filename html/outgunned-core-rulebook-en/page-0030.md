







# **the Nobody** 

_A simple, everyday person, a family man or woman, the neighbor next door, a retiree, or a cashier._ 

Not all Heroes are strong, brave, and trained for action. Some are simple folk who just happened to be in the wrong place at the wrong time. 

Everyday people, the Nobodies didn’t ask to become Heroes but, when action comes a-knocking, they prove to be more determined than they believed themselves.
