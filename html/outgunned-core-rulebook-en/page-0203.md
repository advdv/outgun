

# Prompts About Heroes 

- ♦ **Johnny:** Agent Reed is an undercover police officer tasked with retrieving the blueprints for Project Medusa. His direct superior is the only one who knows anything about his mission, and his partners are also unaware of his true identity. 

- ♦ **Sam:** Samantha is a vehicle enthusiast and a top-notch pilot. She and Victor work together as debt collectors for a less-thanlegal entity. She doesn’t know what’s in the briefcase and doesn't really care. For her, this is just another day on the job. 

- ♦ **Victor:** The life of a bounty hunter is filled with risks and hard choices. A veteran like Victor is well aware of this fact. As such, even if he has smelled the set-up hiding behind this mission, he still decided to take the job. 

- ♦ **Savar:** Doctor Sita Khan is one of the brilliant scientists behind Project Medusa, and she mysteriously vanished a few weeks ago. Her brother Savar fully intends to do everything in his power to bring her back home. After discovering Johnny’s true identity, he decided to also take part in this mission, and the agent was forced to accept his involvement in order not to break cover.
