

# Shots and Cinematic Campaigns 

You can carry out your mission in a single Shot, or during a long Cinematic Campaign. 

A Shot is an adventure (or a fragment of adventure) that unfolds during one gaming Session. 

When playing a single Shot, your mission is rather easy and can be carried out in the span of a few hours by relying on all the tools you found in previous pages. You just need to gather your team, grab your dice, and let the action begin. 

A **Cinematic Campaign** is a string of Shots connected to each-other to create a complex plot that closely follows the classic structure of action movies. 

When you’re playing a Campaign, you need some additional tools, and this Section is exactly where you’ll find them. In the next pages, you’ll find everything you need to keep tension high as the mission progresses. 

# We're in this together 

The Heroes share the same mission, but they’re not necessarily all trying to achieve the same goal. Maybe one of the Heroes is actually trying to save the world, but maybe another is just trying to clear their record. And 

maybe another one has joined the group to save a family member, or because they were paid handsomely for it. When multiple goals come together in one mission, things are bound to get interesting! 

# The Villain 

The Villain is stronger, smarter, better armed, and better prepared than you. And to be fair, they are also more loved by the public. A Hero’s life is an up-hill battle! 

A mission is not complete without a big bad evil guy with an evil plan and all they need to carry it out. In upcoming pages, you’ll find all the tools and tips you’ll need to set the stage for a memorable Villain. 

The only thing that matters is that all the Heroes must be united by the same mission, and that this goal gives them a good reason (or forces them) to trust-each other, at least until the Showdown.
