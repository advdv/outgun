

## **I DON’T THINK SO!** 

When a Hero uses any Feat that requires them to spend some Adrenaline, the Enemies promptly intervene to stop them. 

The Hero loses the action, but doesn’t use up the Feat nor consume Adrenaline. 

## **PILE ON** 

The Enemies pile on the weakest link of the group. The Hero with the least Grit immediately loses 2 Grit. If the Hero has lost all Grit, they do not run the risk of being Left for Dead, but become Tired. If they are already Tired, they become Hurt. If they are already Hurt, they become Broken. 

## **TACKLE** 

An Enemy charges toward a Hero, tackling them head-down. If the Hero fails a Critical Reaction Roll in Brawn+Force, they lose their footing and suffer -1 to their next roll. 

# Choosing Special Actions 

To make your life easier during the session, you can pick the Special Actions the Enemy can rely on beforehand. 

The Enemy won’t necessarily use all the Special Actions you choose, but this will restrict the options you’ll have to consider mid-combat. 

Picking a short list of Special Actions you want the Enemy to have beforehand will help you save some time during combat, and add a sprinkle of spice and healthy tactics to your job as a Director.
