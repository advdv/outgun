

If you wish to stand a chance to succeed in your mission, you should always expect the worst and be ready for anything. Gear up for the mission you don’t want to deal with, not for the stroll you hope to take! 

# Getting Gear 

When filling in your Hero Sheet, you get some starting gear that is granted to you by your **Role** . For example, if you’re an Agent, you start off with a radio, a pistol, and a badge. And if you’re an Ace, you get a ride and a weapon of your choice between pistol or shotgun. Not too shabby! 

While on the mission, you can buy new gear by spending some Cash, by asking a favor from a friend or by... borrowing from your enemies. If they wanted to keep their stuff, they should have thought twice before shooting at you. 

Additionally, if you can rely on the Always Prepared or I’ll Make a Phone Call Feats, you have a way to get some gear quickly and for free. 

# Losing Gear 

You can lose gear as a consequence of your failure, or when you choose to sacrifice it to gain +1 to a roll that you think is extremely important. For example, if you jump out of a racing car and fail, the Director can decide that your beloved pistol was left on your seat. Alternatively, when you’re treating the wounds of a friend, you can always choose to tear away a sleeve of your elegant jacket to use it as bandages and gain +1 to your Heal roll. 

# Bags & Storage 

On your Sheet, you’ll find icons that look like a bag, you can cross out these icons to represent items you are carrying inside a briefcase, a backpack, or some other kind of bag. When you give up or lose your bag, you also lose all items inside of it. 

To be safe, you can also choose to keep some of your items in Storage. Stored gear may be in an actual storehouse, hidden somewhere in your office, or in the trunk of your car. You cannot lose items you keep in Storage (unless someone decides to rob your storing place), but you’ll need quite some time to retrieve them when needed.
