



# Yeah, but what’s the difference? 

The difference between a normal roll and a dangerous roll is that **if you fail a dangerous roll** , you don’t face the usual consequences for a failure. Instead, **you lose a set amount of Grit** depending on the difficulty of the failed roll. 

- ♦ **BASIC:** You lose 1 Grit 

- ♦ **CRITICAL:** You lose 3 Grit 

- ♦ **EXTREME:** You lose 9 Grit 

- ♦ **IMPOSSIBLE:** You lose All Grit 

When you lose an amount of Grit, you must fill in an equal number of **Grit boxes** on your Hero Sheet. 

Once all boxes are full, it means you have lost all Grit. Your next mistake could prove fatal. 

**Victor:** _I open the car door and jump out before things blow up._ 

**Director:** _To do this, you must make a Dangerous Critical Roll._ 

**Victor:** _I roll the dice and... Damnit! Not even a success._ 

**Director:** _The car door breaks off and lands on the asphalt with a fountain of sparks. One second before the car blows up, you jump out and start rolling on the ground. You lose 3 Grit. Not your best landing._
