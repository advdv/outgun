## **INTIMIDATION** 

# Free Re-roll 

Some Feats that grant you a Free Re-roll leave some room for interpretation and require you to use some creativity. For such cases, you should always try to call the Feat in to play when it sounds useful, without getting too greedy. 

For example, the Military Background Feat grants you a Free Re-roll when the long years of hard training can come to your aid. It’s good to take advantage of this benefit when you're coordinating an operation, trying to pass for an enemy soldier, or crawling under some barbed wire. But you can’t call it into play whenever you have a gun in hand. Don't overdo it! 

_You’re an ice-cold tough guy. You’re truly scary._ Gain a Free Re-roll when intimidating or interrogating somebody.
