_A martial artist, a boxer, a batterer, or a self-defense expert._ 

In a world where criminals and law-enforcement compete for who can get the best guns, Fighters have decided to join the fray with bare hands, armed only with their fists and good intentions. Luckily, their fists happen to be registered weapons and their kicks never miss. 

When a man with a rifle meets a Hero with no weapons, the man with the rifle must be careful, for that could be a fearsome Fighter.
