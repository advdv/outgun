# Facing the Villain 

The Villain is more than a simple opponent, they are your sworn nemesis, and the only thing that stands between you and your mission. This is why, when you face a Villain, you must keep a few things in mind: 

- ♦ **The Villain can only lose during the Showdown** and can’t be defeated in earlier Shots. A Villain can meet their bitter end during a Turning Point, only to get replaced by another, stronger and eviler than them. 

- ♦ **All rolls against a Villain are made with -1** , unless you are in a Showdown. 

- ♦ **You can't spend a Spotlight to hurt, capture, or swindle the Villain** , unless you are in a Showdown. 

- ♦ **Not all Villains are fearsome Enemies** in combat. Those who aren’t can rely on a right hand who is ready to make your life difficult. 

# The Villain’s Right Hand 

A good Villain can always rely on one or two right hand people, Boss type Enemies, or Bad Guys with a high-level Template. 

The Villain’s right hand is an Enemy in all respects, but they can only be defeated during a Turning Point or Showdown. If dealt with during any other Shot, they will always find a way to return. 

Outgunned. Outnumbered. Outplanned. 

Let’s be clear, some people will turn up in twenty to have words with a guy smaller than them. Around here, we call them enemies. You, on the other hand, 

you’re a Hero. You always pick fights with people bigger than you. 

As such, the Villain is always stronger and better prepared than you, and their goons always have the numbers advantage. 

Don’t worry, that means you're on the right side of this!
